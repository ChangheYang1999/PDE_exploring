The selection function accounts for twelve distinct result paths: eight baselines, G and L against H and S in both physical assignments, plus four R controls against H and S. The orientation of S controls is correct. S is the recorded candidate, so the code assigns the member seat as one minus S's candidate seat. Payoff is then extracted directly from the engine winner at that member seat; it does not reuse S's outcome sign. Thus an S/L win becomes an L/S loss, and an S/G draw remains a draw. HP is reordered consistently. Each physical seat receives its own two-by-two payoff table; neither paired averaging nor role pooling can conceal a contrary assignment.
