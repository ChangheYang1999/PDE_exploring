# Reading order

1. [REVIEW.md](REVIEW.md) gives the source finding, retained-history limits and suggested correction.
2. [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) states and proves the exact conditional control-flow result.
3. [EVIDENCE.json](EVIDENCE.json) binds source/defaults/specifications, the captured entry and retained records.
4. [inspect_source_records.py](inspect_source_records.py) is the standard-library inspection script.
5. [seal.json](seal.json) records actual preparation and seal times and authenticates the new files. Earlier seals remain unchanged.
