# Early-wall round-four purchase ablation

## Exact empirical questions

The control is EarlyWallGuard(witness="guard00"): the fixed round-one wall route, splash(1,3) in round three, one tier4 in round four if affordable, remaining opening cash spent on factories, and the frozen Fortress38 continuation from round five. Change only the round-four minion type to tier3 or omit that purchase. The resulting unspent gold flows through the existing factory loop. This intervention therefore compares offensive pressure with additional factories; it does not hold the factory count fixed.

Evaluate both variants against the same seven fixed policies in both physical assignments: best_v9e, V33C, P5TNUBM, frozen Fortress41, hp_handoff with lead_from=8 and deadline=12, and the adaptive and forced-early Fortress38 delegated counters. The complete manifest contains 28 new games. Reuse the fourteen control conditions already declared in early_wall_policy and early_wall_transfer only after their audits finish and their exact source archives are verified.

H1: omitting the tier4 purchase changes both control losses against the declared hp_handoff policy to a draw or win. H2: at least one variant has no W/D/L regression on all fourteen controls and at least one strict improvement. Use L < D < W, and report every condition regardless of these claims. A higher mean HP difference is not enough to establish either claim.

## Intervention and component argument

The new policy inherits the original witness, persistent continuation and diagnostics. Its decision body retains the same ordering and action filter. The sole policy parameter changes the optional minion purchase in round four. With round4_type="tier4", the conditional, price check and submitted action coincide with the original. With any parameter, decisions in rounds one through three coincide under equal observations. From round five the same deterministic continuation is called on the actual observation. Observations may already differ because changing the fourth-round purchase changes combat and income; identical continuation code does not imply identical subsequent actions.

Before the complete comparison, check the tier4 implementation against the original through five legal rounds against four declared simple growth/strike schedules and both assignments. Verify exact actions, records, legal submissions and unchanged observations. Check the two interventions on the same prefixes, retain their round-four spending and post-round states, and report any filtered action. This finite implementation check supplements the branch argument; it does not prove equality for all possible Python inputs.
