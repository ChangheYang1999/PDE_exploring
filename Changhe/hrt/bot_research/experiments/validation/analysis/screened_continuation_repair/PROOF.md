## Why the prefix is expected to agree

The subclass changes only the delegate returned from round7 onward. The parent opening and five-wall guard determine every earlier submitted action. A fresh deterministic opposing policy receives the same public observations, up to labels. The original target does not inspect names, as separately tested on the reached histories. Induction over the first six rounds therefore predicts equal normalized records. The audit checks the actual complete prefix, including enemy actions, instead of assuming this property from final HP.

A continuation may have internal state. It is constructed at the start of the game and first called on the real round7 observation; its initialization is not advanced through a fictional earlier history. Full-game reproduction must instantiate the same policy and advance it on each actual observation. This makes the switch reproducible, but it does not imply that a delegate designed for a standard start is optimal or safe after the switch.
