# Transfer of a fixed public-budget counter

## Exact statement and hypotheses

Fix the unchanged balanced engine, standard initial state, and each exact target specification in the three budget-transfer-001 declarations. Targets are P4TNUB, fortress v36, fortress v37, the installed best_v9e, and lead8. Fix the previously declared BudgetHandoff policy with one tier4 purchase in round5, the screened opening, handoff6 or7, budget fraction1/2 or1, and a cap of120 hypothetical new copies. No parameter or model is changed after observing a target's result. Each pair is played in both physical assignments.

The empirical question is whether the four fixed budget counters transfer their P5TNUBM wins to these five targets. A win against one target does not imply a win against another. The claim is OPEN until every declared condition is accounted for and its outcome is checked with the original engine. Results must state losses and draws as well as wins.

## Argument and dependencies

BudgetHandoff uses the public opponent gold and active roster to form the same finite pure-tier extensions for every target. It does not receive a target identity or construct an opponent policy clone. Legal action filtering uses the original owner-local validator, on a fresh reconstruction of the observing player's state. Inductively, each accepted action updates the reconstruction exactly as the original engine would update that player's state; the returned subsequence is legal. This is the same construction proved in ../budget_handoff/PROOF.md. The later factory purchases are also checked against the already updated state.

The policies and initial state determine two complete original-engine games per target-counter pair, subject to any clock-limited search in a target. Replaying all submitted actions checks the resulting trajectory, final HP and engine-declared outcome. A second run of the counter on the recorded observations checks whether its named policy generated the recorded actions, while retaining all detected differences. Identical normalized seat-pair histories show agreement only for those observed runs. They do not convert the games into independent samples or establish that a target with a clock cutoff is universally deterministic.

Fortress targets retain their bounded-time planner and its resulting load sensitivity. The installed best_v9e is pinned by full source hash, and every loaded source is checked against the archived closure. lead8 keeps its own previously declared forecast model even when the budget counter is the actual opponent. The budget policy never sees or changes that model. The target source, counter source, exact JSON data and manifests are dependencies of the comparison.

## Scope

This is a finite transfer experiment, not a new theorem of game strength. The legal/public-input claim is independent of whether the counter wins. In particular, defending only after a fixed deadline can lose when the opponent already has a lead; the policy has no promised comeback mechanism. All conditions are disclosed development tests. Reserved pool C is not used.
