# Transfer of the emergency continuation

Written 2026-10-31T16:46:54.657792+00:00, before the transfer games.

## Statement and exact conditions

The empirical hypothesis is that the damage-triggered EmergencyRouteSelector avoids every defeat avoided by the preceding RouteGatedSelector on the 45 previously declared targets, and repairs its six losing route-builder settings. This is a development transfer test, not a held-out validation. The complete target union is the 24 targets in route-selector-001, the 16 targets in route-challenge-a001 and route-challenge-b001, and the five in route-transfer-001. Each is tested in both physical assignments, for 90 new games. Every result is retained.

The new counter uses the unchanged default adaptive predicate and emergency_rule="damage", emergency_from=5. Its display name is kept as route_adaptive to permit exact public-history comparison with the prior campaign. This label does not identify the implementation: the manifest and source attestations do. Target names, parameters and engine settings also remain the same. The three partitions are operational batches only and are not independently chosen evaluation sets.

## Informal argument and limitations

The policy's legitimate-information and common-opening argument is in ../emergency_after_strike/PROOF.md. Prior to round five, the emergency condition is disabled. In the growth branch it is never used. In the early branch, it can change actions only if it activates before the old positive-HP-lead condition. Thus any complete history for which it never activates should agree with the old policy, subject to exact reproducibility of shared planning routines. Observing such equality is a control check, not a general proof of reproducibility under arbitrary clock load.

Positive current-roster damage does not guarantee a useful defense, and zero damage does not exclude a hidden purchase. An emergency purchase can reduce later growth or offense. Therefore neither safety nor improved results on the 45 targets follows analytically. All outcomes must come from complete original-engine games. The changed trajectories, their first divergence, activation timing and outcome differences will be reported, including regressions. W/D/L rank is win > draw > loss for the counter; raw HP margins are secondary and may exceed initial base HP.

## Dependencies and verification

The declaration pins the old manifests and new source closure. Each full game requires strict result accounting, original-engine replay, data/source identity and fresh regeneration of counter decisions. Old and new results are matched by target specification and physical assignment. Exactly matching canonical histories are reported separately from identical outcomes. The stored 90 pairs permit independent checking. Existing best_v9e and its final pool-C evidence remain unchanged. This experiment gives no global optimality claim.
