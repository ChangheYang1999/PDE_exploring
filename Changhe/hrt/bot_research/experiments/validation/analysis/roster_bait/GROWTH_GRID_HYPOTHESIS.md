# Growth timing around the roster bait

The initial five no-turret bait conditions all lost against economy21 and
fortress33. The one-tier2 case nevertheless left fortress33 at88 HP. Test the
falsifiable hypothesis that changing the growth horizon defeats at least one
of these policies while retaining the same simple grow/flood structure.

The declared grid has growth_rounds=4,5,6,7,8,9 and round-two bait lists empty,
one tier1, two tier1, one tier2, and one tier2 followed by two tier1. There is
no turret. The flood is tier5, factory_cap=2000 and purchase_cap=1000. All thirty
settings are tested against both policies and in both assignments,120 games.
The existing growth6 points are reproducibility controls, not additional
independent conditions. A grid search is development data and cannot support
a held-out strength claim. If failures appear, later confirmation must name
the selected conditions and separate them from untested ones.

The earlier roster-visibility lemma applies because the fixed round-two
purchases are affordable and the minimum growth horizon is four. Component
checks establish that affordability on the actual prefixes. The full games
require source stability, strict accounting and original-engine replay.
Fortress33's finite wall-clock planner remains an execution limitation.
