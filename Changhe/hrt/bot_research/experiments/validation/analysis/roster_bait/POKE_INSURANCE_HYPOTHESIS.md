# Cheap-poke insurance and the bait loss

The completed V18FT trace against one round-two tier1 buys a gun1 and splash
in round three and has twelve factories at round six; the empty grower
control has thirty-one. This is a descriptive association. The bait also
affects damage, bounties, visible mandatory defense and opponent factories.
It is not yet evidence that speculative insurance causes the loss.

Test five candidate definitions: actual V18FT, V18FT with poke_insurance=false,
V18FT with poke_until=2, actual V24G, and V24G with poke_insurance=false.
Keep all ten previous bait/gun conditions and add four budget_timing controls:
tier1 at round2, tier2 at round2, tier3 at round3, and tier4 at round5, all with
budget_fraction=0.5. Both assignments give 140 games. All conditions are
retained regardless of outcome. Controls are intentional repeated executions.

Hypothesis: disabling cheap-poke insurance fixes the no-turret one/two-tier1
bait losses. The four early-wave controls test the separate hypothesis that
this does not introduce new losses. Both may be refuted. The interventions
change policy parameters for the complete game; they do not hold histories
fixed after the first changed action. Any mechanism attribution needs the
recorded first difference and relevant construction/finance changes.

The opponents and parameter choices are development data. No reserve-C
condition is used. Full-game claims require strict accounting, frozen sources,
legal actions and original-engine replay.
