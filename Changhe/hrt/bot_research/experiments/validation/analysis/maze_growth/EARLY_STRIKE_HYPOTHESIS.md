# Early strikes against the passive exemption

Declared before these full-game results on 2026-10-31. The existing growth
recurrence and construction checks in PROOF.md apply. This is a development
hypothesis, not an additional proved strategic claim.

A grower with no walls, turrets or bait can remain passive until it buys its
first attack. Test growth through rounds2,3,4 and then spend every round's
available gold on tier2, tier3, tier4 or tier5. Purchases below affordability
are empty; exact first purchase rounds will be read from the logs. Compare
the frozen best_v9e, v24 P5, and v25 R5, with both assignments, retaining all
72 conditions. The hypothesis is that removing speculative insurance before
round6 could allow an earlier damaging strike that best_v9e stops. Later
mandatory defense and lost factory growth can still defeat this counter.

These opponents and parameters are chosen from known development mechanisms.
They are not reserve-pool observations. Equal deterministic trajectories
across variants count as transfer checks, not independent strength evidence.
