# Transfer of guarded counters to the turret-passivity revision

Declared before the transfer games on 2026-10-31. Compare P5TN, P5TNR and
P5TN with current attacks against four disclosed development opponents:
the original guards R4/R6, the screened guard R9, and guard handoffs H12
and H16. Both player assignments, standard start,24 games. The conjecture
is that ignoring the early guard turret changes the opening enough to
prevent the earlier failed-attack sequence; the competing conjecture is
that the same permanent adaptation failure persists. Every condition is
hash pinned, replayed on the unchanged engine and compared with matched
controls where available. The handoff defender retains a wall-clock gun
planning cutoff, so repeated actions are measured, not assumed identical.
No held-out pool or universal strategy claim is involved.
