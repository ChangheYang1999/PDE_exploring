# Development hypotheses declared before selector games

The public three-tier1 opening distinguishes the two lead candidates from
five current economy/fortress candidates in the known seven-policy pool.
The one-tier1 bait arm has already beaten the two lead candidates; the
staged-guard arm has already beaten economy21 and fortress33. Hypothesis:
choosing between these arms from the public round2 roster beats all seven.
This is an in-sample development hypothesis, including the anticipated
transfer to economy23, economy25RM and fortress34. It is not validation.

Test all seven against the selector on the standard start, both assignments,
14 games. Compare complete histories to the matching recorded fixed-arm
controls. Repeated controls will be identified explicitly.

Stress hypothesis: the rule may fail when opening rosters change without
changing the rest of a candidate's policy. Test fortress34 with round1_poke=3,
economy21 with r1_units=3, and V18FT with r1_poke=2 against the selector and
both fixed arms, both assignments,18 games. These parameter changes are
chosen before their results. They are new development conditions, not a
reserved or independently sampled opponent distribution.

The lead's new passive_until=5 policy is an additional development transfer
condition after its exact source is pinned. No poolC condition is consumed.
