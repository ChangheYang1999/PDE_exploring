Record every action and its exact source closure. During these histories candidate and opponent calls are skipped, so the resulting games are continuation tests. Do not merge those scores into the standard-start score.

At candidate freeze regenerate all jobs from the unchanged manifest and record the source closure. Each condition runs in a fresh interpreter under the unchanged balanced Engine. Replay every selected action history through a fresh original Engine and compare every record. Regenerate candidate actions independently and require exact action equality. Check complete pairs and canonical player-relative trajectories; identical histories count once when describing behavioral diversity.

Audit normalized parameter equality and exact prescribed-history equality against the recorded pre-campaign validation jobs and recognized team result streams.
