# Pressure before the handoff

Declared after the first two always-current H10 executions lost and before
these 32 games. The conjecture is that pressure before the round10 defense
can preserve a lead or end the game. Starting from P5TNUF3, change only one
parameter per candidate: open_share 0.1, 0.25 or 0.5; attack_from 3, 4 or 5;
or attack_share 0.75 or 1.0. Test H10 and H12, both seats.

The source's open_share branch attacks an opponent with no turret before
attack_from, after the catch-up branch. The attack_from parameter advances
the regular attack. The attack_share parameter also changes early catch-up
spending if that branch fires. Thus these are separate policy interventions,
not interchangeable names for the same treatment. Costs can delay factories
and defense. Cross-family regressions are required before recommendation.

Compare full histories to the repeated P5TNUF3 controls in h10-transfer-001.
All conditions are disclosed development conditions. F34 has a planning
clock cutoff, so paired equality is an observed fact to check, not assumed.
