# Extending the guard check at the tier2 threshold

The completed unguarded grid has one economy21 draw: lead_opening, growth5,
round2 tier2 bait, both assignments. Both bases are destroyed in round9.
The single-cell design check found that the lead_opening splash guard has
70 per-copy exposure, exactly tier2 HP, while the empty and cash_rank_1
versions have only50 and60. Thus the lead-opening guard excludes persistent
tier2 damage after installation, as well as tier1.

Hypothesis: that threshold can turn the unguarded draw into a counter win,
or produce other wins against economy21 and fortress33. Test growth through
rounds4/5/6, guard in rounds3/4, bait none/tier2 in round2, on lead_opening.
That is12 opponents against two candidates in both assignments:48 games.
The parameter extension is selected from development evidence. It is not a
new held-out population. Against the matching unguarded controls where they
exist, compare outcomes and the first changed purchases; do not infer that
any improvement is exclusively caused by blocking tier2, since the guard
also changes cash, route and the opponent's subsequent decisions.
