# Transferring the delayed guard counter to later economic policies

The completed standard-start grids identify twelve guard4 settings on three
layouts, growth5/6 and bait none/tier2. Their economy21 outcomes are already
recorded. Economy23 changes re-plan adoption; economy25's proposed RM setting
also changes whether adopted-layout exclusions count as foreign and raises
attack caps. Those changes may or may not occur on these histories.

Test all twelve settings against recommended frozen economy23 and proposed
frozen economy25 RM, both assignments, 48 matches. The hypothesis is that the
later changes do not remove the delayed-guard counter on standard starts.
Compare complete trajectories against economy21 where parameters otherwise
match. These are selected development counterexamples, not held-out evidence
and not a claim that the later policies are weaker overall.
