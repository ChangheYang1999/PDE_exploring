# Earlier tier4 waves after a delayed guard

The first guarded grid has seven of eight guard-round4 settings defeating
economy21, but all sixteen settings lose to fortress33. In the empty layout,
growth6, no-bait trace, fortress33 has48 factories after round6 and survives
the first two tier5 waves before completing enough splash defense. The grower
has89 factories but has stopped investing and cannot force further damage.

Hypothesis: a cheaper tier4 wave at an earlier horizon can exploit the period
before fortress33 reaches its tier4 splash threshold. Test all three selected
layouts, growth through rounds4/5/6, guard in round4, and no bait or one tier2
in round2. Compare actual V18FT, economy21 and fortress33, both assignments:
18 opponents and108 games. These are additional development settings, chosen
after inspecting the tier5 failures, not independent held-out opponents.
The matching tier5 controls for growth5/6 are already recorded; growth4 has
matching controls only for lead_opening against economy21 and fortress33.
The same full-policy comparison limitations apply: tier, costs, quantities,
subsequent reactions and income all vary after the first changed purchase.
