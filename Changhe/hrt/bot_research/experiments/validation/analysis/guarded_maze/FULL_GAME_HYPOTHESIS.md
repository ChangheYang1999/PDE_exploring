# Delayed splash-guard experiment

The unguarded growers can die to persistent cheap rosters before their heavy
waves finish the opponent. Hypothesis: a single splash added in round3 or4
can prevent this against the economic and fortress policies, while retaining
enough factories to threaten their heavy-copy defense.

Before full games, require exact data checks and dense-roster original-combat
checks for the selected guard. Then compare empty and searched cash_rank_1
wall layouts; guard rounds3/4; growth horizons5/6; and bait[]/[tier2]. Against
V18FT, recommended E21 and F33 in both assignments, this is96 declared cases.
These are development conditions, with no reserve-C use. Every unguarded
counterpart was declared in the earlier maze grid; pairing uses the full
configuration rather than outcome-based selection.
