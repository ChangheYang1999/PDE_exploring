Conversely, [the econ17/fortress25 repeat comparison](../../runs/validation/e17-f25-repeat-comparison-001.json) shows that four cited standard games agree exactly after correcting player perspective; they do not demonstrate clock-induced divergence.

All selected completions in the summaries above passed source checks, legality checks, and full action replay through the original engine. Replay establishes that the submitted actions produce the recorded game. It does not, by itself, prove that a bot generated those actions from permitted information; source inspection, fresh-process isolation, and the separate policy/forecast checks address that question.

Every failed or invalid attempt remains in accounting. The known-prefix development campaign recorded a round-49 timeout under a450-second bound.
