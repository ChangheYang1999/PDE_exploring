# Clarifications after the independent audit

These details supplement the unchanged proof and fix its scope, without adding a numerical claim.

1. In the canonical outer modal formulas, U1/x=-sum P_n cos(2n theta)-sum Q_n sin(theta)sin(2n theta)/cos(theta), and U2/y=-sum P_n cos(2n theta)+sum Q_n cos(theta)sin(2n theta)/sin(theta). The divided factors are finite trigonometric polynomials because sin(2n theta) is divisible by both sin(theta) and cos(theta). Proof 117 supplies termwise cylinder bounds, hence both quotients are O(r^(k-1)); their first Cartesian derivatives are O(r^(k-2)). This explicitly supplies the differentiated bound in the transport-image membership check. Take the far radius beyond the actual outer junction, rather than presuming a homogeneous formula already at radius1.

2. J(f_b)=0 means the ordinary local cubic jet. The forcing is smooth and zero on a neighbourhood of the physical origin, so that jet is unambiguously zero even though the forcing is not compactly supported. No extension of a nonclosable graph functional is being asserted.

3. The exact integrated drift bound can be taken as delta_Y=2C Y^(k-1)/(ell(1-k)), with a_Y=exp(-delta_Y), B_Y=exp(delta_Y), after assigning the same bound to each component. The factor r_t<=x_t+y_t<=2 B_Y Y exp(ell t) then follows for0<x<1<=Y. All constants are qualitative.

4. The proof establishes infinitely many independent cosets; the optional strengthening of their cardinality is unnecessary. It does not identify the range of F0 or assert that the operator is not a valid closed realization. The exact conclusion is failure of surjectivity and Fredholmness on this common Y target.

5. The independent audit's illustrative fitted exponents are numerical sanity checks, not certified quantities used here. The proof uses the exact algebra Q_b/ell=2-b-k and ell k=1.
