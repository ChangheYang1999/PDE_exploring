For a smooth decaying probe phi, the weak identity and Cauchy-Schwarz
bound give

 |I| <= D ||h||_2 + Hbar ||h||_2^2,

       + ||phi grad rho||_2,

Since D and Hbar are nonnegative, a certified positive pairing gives

 ||h||_2 >= q = 2|I|/(D + sqrt(D^2 + 4 Hbar |I|)).

If the numerical certificate gives q > l, every correction under these
hypotheses has norm greater than l. The review uses the already accepted
full-plane certificate and its independent replay. It does not repeat
all integrations. No table value is an upper bound on the distance to
stationary profiles or a maximum over all probes.

## Directed numerical displays

A saved decimal ball [m +/- e] supplies rational endpoints m-e and m+e.
Parse both decimals as exact rational numbers, with e >= 0. A printed
strict lower floor b is licensed by an accepted strict floor l if b <= l.
It can also be licensed by b below a separately accepted lower endpoint
for q. If b is above the upper endpoint for q, that certificate does not
license b. This refutes the claim of support from that certificate; it
does not show that an actual correction has norm at most b.

floor 367590231030927/250000000000000000. Its recomputed q lies between
0.00147036092412370996622 and 0.00147036092412370996623. The printed

rational floor are safe strict lower displays. N4-328 already checked
this issue in numerics_v11. Repetition in a later report is a new
transcription instance, not new mathematical evidence.

To compare a distance floor with a correction-ball ceiling, use a valid
upper ceiling. In particular, the original graded family has
||h||_2 <= 25/54684. The displayed upper scalar ceilings 2.8496e-6 for
the sharpw family and 3.7930e-6 for the TB-sharp family have separately
accepted exact brackets. Ratios reported as lower factors must be
rounded downward after division by these upper ceilings. A printed
threshold marked approximate is not substituted for its exact registered
value in a proof of smallness.

If a sufficient forcing condition S implies existence E, failure of S
alone gives no implication concerning failure of E. The point and weak
certificates are separate necessary conditions and can exclude their

stationary IPM profiles. Likewise, a bound in L2 does not exclude a
correction class with a decaying weight that need not embed in L2.

is used to supply a dyadic verdict.

## Background margins and homogeneous negative norms

The original (B-L2) numerical test in the frozen theory defines kappa
by its explicit whole-plane Hilbert-Schmidt upper estimate and requires
c+1-theta-kappa >= 2. A failure of this particular estimate is a failure
of the stated test. It does not lower-bound the actual signed commutator
or refute a stronger compression estimate. The signed compression route
for candidate008 is recorded separately and still requires theta and
its other hypotheses. The trace must preserve that distinction.

For H = ||Lambda^(-1) F||_2 and an orthogonal sine-mode expansion
F = sum F_n, the mode Hardy estimate may prove
H^2 <= B^2 = sum n^(-2) ||r F_n||_2^2. Complete construction of every
nonzero mode and every radial branch cross term is required for this
upper bound. A subset gives a partial sum of B^2, which is not an upper
bound for H^2. Even a complete accepted B is an upper bound, not a
certificate of H=B. The accepted007 Green enclosure has a different,
two-sided meaning. Neither its precision nor its source acceptance
transfers to candidate016 or017 without their own verification.

stored-versus-decoded compression convention. Hashes do not establish

claim that stronger provenance.
