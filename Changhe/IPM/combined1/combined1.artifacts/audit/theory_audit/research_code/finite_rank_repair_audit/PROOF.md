# High regularity after finite-rank removal using the raw low norm

## Statement and exact hypotheses

Let L and X be the low L2 space and the high X_s space from the audited T4-051/T4-057 framework, with continuous inclusion X into L. Let A be its background closed generator, with consistent low and high realizations and a dense high generator core. Write

    b=||h||L2, b_s=||J^s h||2, a=||D J^s h||2,
    P=||omega D^2 J^s h||2.

For lambda>=0 assume the generator-domain estimates for (lambda-A)h=g are

    (m1+lambda)a <= g1+q b_s,
    (m2+lambda)P^2 <= (g2+u1 a+v1 b_s)P+40a^2,
    b_s <= b+s a,

where m2>0, alpha=m1-sq>0, q,v1,u1>=0 and g1=||D J^s g||2, g2=||omega D^2 J^s g||2. The original finite-time high generator construction is assumed, not deduced from these stationary estimates alone.

Let K h=sum_(j=1)^N psi_j <phi_j,h> with psi_j in X and phi_j in L. Let D=A-K on the same domain. Suppose its low resolvent exists for every real lambda>=0 and

    ||(lambda-D)^-1||_(L->L) <= 1/(beta_D+lambda), beta_D>0.

Define finite constants

    d1=sum_j ||D J^s psi_j||2 ||phi_j||2,
    d2=sum_j ||omega D^2 J^s psi_j||2 ||phi_j||2,
    k1=u1+s v1.

Then every low resolvent preserves X. In its high stationary estimates one may use

    (alpha+lambda)a <= f1+(q+d1)b,
    (m2+lambda)P^2 <= [f2+k1a+(v1+d2)b]P+40a^2,             (1)
    b <= f0/(beta_D+lambda).

No additional condition alpha-sd1>0 is required. Constants d1,d2 can be large, but must be finite and accounted for in the quantitative bound.

## Proof

The finite-rank map is bounded L->X and hence bounded X->X and L->L. Bounded perturbation preserves the high generator with the same high domain and its consistency with the low realization. For h in that domain, (lambda-D)h=f gives (lambda-A)h=f-Kh. The triangle inequality and low Cauchy-Schwarz imply

    g1 <= f1+d1 b, g2 <= f2+d2 b.

Substitute these into the assumed A rows and use b_s<=b+s a only on the original q and v1 terms. This gives(1) exactly. In particular the forcing generated by K is controlled against the raw b, so replacing it by b_s would be an avoidable loss.

Together with the low resolvent bound, (1) gives a high a priori inverse estimate locally uniform in lambda>=0. Indeed first bound b, then a, then solve the positive quadratic for P, and finally b_s<=b+s a. At sufficiently large positive lambda the high generator resolvent already exists. Continue toward zero by the high resolvent identity: the set of invertible real parameters is open, and its locally uniform inverse bound makes it closed along each compact interval. A Cauchy sequence of high inverse images converges in X; the closed graph gives the limiting inverse and surjectivity. Uniqueness and consistency follow from the low inverse. This proves preservation of X and the displayed estimates. Approximation of f in X passes the inequalities to all f in X.

## Relation to the previous audit and scope

T4-059 correctly found an extra alpha-sd1 positivity condition in the coarser argument that first bounded K against b_s. T1's revised raw-L2 argument avoids that loss and is confirmed here. Thus TP-1b's high-column regularity is repaired under the exact generator and low-resolvent assumptions above. Quantitative use still needs a finite-matrix singular-value margin and a certified K_L for the Woodbury inverse. Merely knowing that the matrix is nonsingular is insufficient for the specified closure tolerance. No such matrix certificate is supplied by this analytic repair.
