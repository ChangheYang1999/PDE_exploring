# Arsenovic and Mateljevic: checked primary statement

Primary source: On Ahlfors-Beurling Operator, Ukrainian Mathematical Bulletin18(3),292-302(2021), DOI10.37069/1810-3200-2021-18-3-1. Section2, pp293-297, was read in the actual returned web.run PDF extraction. The local curl responses remain HTML and are not the source text.

Let K(x)=Omega(x/|x|)|x|^(-n), with Omega continuously differentiable on the sphere and of spherical mean zero. Use the circular principal value. For the paper's continuous increasing concave majorant omega, omega(0)=0, assume, for0<delta<=1,

    integral_0^delta omega(t)dt/t <= A1 omega(delta),
    integral_delta^1 omega(t)dt/t^2 <= A2 omega(delta)/delta.

Theorem1, p295, states: for every R>0 there exists C=C(R,n,Omega,omega) bounding [Tf]_omega by C[f]_omega whenever supp(f) is contained in B(0,R). The input may be complex valued. No numerical C is supplied. The proof occupies pp295-297. The Ahlfors-Beurling kernel is explicitly included on p293.

Classification: qualitative compact-support modulus boundedness. This is not a sharp norm formula or a numerical Schauder constant. The later application sections were not studied. Earlier unsupported access claims remain retracted; this acceptance rests on the new actual tool response.
