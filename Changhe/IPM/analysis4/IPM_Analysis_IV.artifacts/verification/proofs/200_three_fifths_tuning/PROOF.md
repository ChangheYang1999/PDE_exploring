# Independent tuning certificate at delta = 3/5

Status: PROVED by the complete argument below and the exact certificates admitted in V410 and V416. All four real profiles converge on an exterior region; explicit radius bounds are separate.

## Exact target

Set delta=3/5, c=5/2, and f1(theta)=sin(theta)+b sin(3 theta). The first stream resonance is n=10, with stream frequency4 and density compatibility frequency3. Let O(b) be the frequency3 coefficient of f10 generated by the nonresonant recurrence. Let P be the positive-leading primitive integer multiple of O.

The target is to certify that P is irreducible of degree10, has exactly four real roots, and that each such root has nonzero tuning response at every later resonance and a nonzero limiting response. Under the already proved rational tuning theorem, these properties give a convergent pure density/stream series with all required weighted derivatives and a common potential. Since h=3 in delta=2h/Q, Q=10, the potential has no exceptional logarithmic correction. This certificate alone gives no sharp radius.

## Independent recurrence

The implementation uses signed Laurent Fourier coefficients and Cartesian differentiation. Its density coefficient is formed from

    f_n = [2/(3(n-1))] sum_(i+j=n) (g_i,x f_j,y - g_i,y f_j,x),

where the radial powers are included in the Cartesian field representation. The Poisson equation is -Delta g_i=partial_x f_i. Each nonresonant inverse is checked by differentiation. These identities are independently derived in topic01.

At a resonant order n=10k, a unit free stream perturbation is sin((6k-2)theta). Propagating its affine effect through the next10 density orders defines sigma_k(b), the frequency(6k+3) coefficient at n+10. The affine index argument of topics194 and198 shows that this response depends only on f1 through f10 and g1 through g9. Homogeneity in the leading profile gives degree at most10 in b.

For k>=7, the two signed carriers are disjoint: the carrier magnitude is6k-2>=40, whereas the density frequency offsets through10 steps have magnitude at most39. Thus a rational symbolic expression in k from the signed-carrier calculation equals the physical response on this entire integer tail. Direct evaluations at7 and8 provide independent finite consistency checks of that expression, in addition to the support proof. Smaller k are computed with ordinary signed integer frequencies, so collisions and cancellations are included exactly.

## Two-coefficient nondivisibility certificate

Write O0 and O10 for the constant and degree10 coefficients of O. Let s0(k) and s10(k) be the corresponding coefficients of sigma_k. They are obtained by performing the response recurrence with the pure leading profiles sin(theta) and sin(3 theta), respectively. Define

    C(k) = O10 s0(k) - O0 s10(k).

If sigma_k vanished at a root of the irreducible degree10 polynomial P, its degree bound would force sigma_k to be a scalar multiple of O, including the zero polynomial. Such a multiple has C(k)=0. Hence C(k) different from zero excludes a response zero at every root simultaneously.

A rational expression C=N/D is certified nonzero on its integer tail by shifting to an explicit integer K and checking that every coefficient of either N(K+x) or its negative is nonnegative, with positive constant term, and likewise for D(K+x). Each resulting polynomial is then strictly positive for x>=0. All remaining finitely many integers are checked directly. If this sufficient two-coefficient test vanishes at an isolated index, the full fixed-index response polynomial must instead be computed and its greatest common divisor with P certified constant. No conclusion is drawn merely from a failed sufficient test.

The general response-limit theorem gives a finite polynomial sigma_infinity(b) of degree at most10. The independently computed limits of s0 and s10 determine C_infinity. If this number is nonzero, the same irreducibility argument excludes a zero of sigma_infinity at any root of P. For a fixed real root, convergence to a nonzero limit together with nonzero values at every finite index implies a strictly positive infimum of the absolute responses. This is the exact hypothesis needed by the tuning theorem.

## Root certificates

The polynomial is generated independently before comparison with the theory part's polynomial. A primitive polynomial with nonzero leading coefficient modulo a prime is irreducible over the rationals if its reduction is irreducible of the same degree. The finite-field certificate checks the requisite Frobenius power and greatest-common-divisor identities, and multiplies factors back to the input reduction. A rational Sturm chain counts all real roots and certifies one root in each retained disjoint interval. Consequently the list omits no real roots. All printed root decimals are illustrations of those rational intervals.

## Scope and dependencies

The algebraic argument above is independent of numerical growth fits. It proves a convergence hypothesis, not an optimal convergence radius or a whole-plane extension. It relies on the already reviewed rational tuning theorem, the response degree/limit theorem, and the independent Cartesian derivative identities.
