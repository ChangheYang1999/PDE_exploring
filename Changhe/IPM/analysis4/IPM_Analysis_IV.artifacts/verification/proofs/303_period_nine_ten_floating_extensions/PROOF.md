# Finite floating extensions at periods nine and ten

Status: protocol written before the new arithmetic checks. No infinite-order claim is made.

## Statement and hypotheses

Let Q be 9 or 10, let delta=2/Q, and fix one of the ten Q=9 or fourteen Q=10 compatibility roots already isolated by the exact calculations. The producer uses a rounded root and floating arithmetic, with a positive amplitude that is an integer power of two. For each completed extension from order306 to612 at Q=9, or from order300 to600 at Q=10, compare the printed unit-amplitude norm and tuning values with the archived baseline and recompute every finite least-squares statistic. An incomplete output is not a completed case.

The assertion to be verified is an exact statement about the printed finite datasets. Agreement is reported component by component, including every discrepancy. No tolerance is substituted for exact equality. This assertion does not enclose the high-order coefficients at the exact algebraic root.

## Notation and argument

For each complete block jQ<n<=(j+1)Q, define x_j=(j+1)Q and let y_j be the largest printed base-ten norm logarithm in that block. Apply this separately to density and stream. The last printed order and last complete block are recorded, since the completion banner can be one order ahead of the printed norm table.

For each lower endpoint L in {100,150,200,300,400,500}, retain the blocks with x_j>=L when at least four such blocks exist. Write m for the number of retained blocks. The unique affine least-squares minimizer has

    a = (m sum(x_j y_j) - sum(x_j) sum(y_j))
        / (m sum(x_j^2) - (sum(x_j))^2),
    b = (sum(y_j) - a sum(x_j))/m.

The denominator is positive because the x_j are distinct. Exact rational arithmetic on the printed decimal inputs gives a, b and the squared RMS error exactly. Directed evaluation encloses A=10^a and the apparent physical radius A^(Q/2). Comparing slopes at the same lower endpoint gives the relative change 10^(a_new-a_old)-1; it is not the same comparison as distance outside the old range over several lower endpoints.

The baseline and extension are normalized by their own homogeneous amplitude powers. Exact powers of two introduce no initial binary amplitude rounding, but later floating operations and printed logarithms can still differ. Record the two amplitudes, all common norm entries, all common residual/response/harmonic entries, and every difference. Equal printed values prove only equal printed values. Nonzero differences remain visible even if small.
