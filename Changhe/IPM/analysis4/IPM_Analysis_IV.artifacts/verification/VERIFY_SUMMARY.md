## Main conclusion

**Nontrivial convergent infinite far-field series exist.** If 0 < delta < 1 is irrational and delta/2 is Brjuno, every nonzero finite real odd sine polynomial generates a real analytic exterior solution. The density, stream and common potential converge absolutely, with every fixed weighted Cartesian derivative, on each closed exterior strictly beyond the sufficient radius. Analytic leading data are also covered, in a smaller angular strip. The proof bounds products of small divisors along the recursion trees. See [13](proofs/13_lead_theory_audit/PROOF.md), [14](proofs/14_analytic_data/PROOF.md) and [76](proofs/76_error_level_majorant/PROOF.md).

At delta = 1/sqrt(5), unit sin(theta) has sufficient exterior R = 2^26, and the unnormalized Candidate 007 leading profile has R = 2^42. The same radii hold at the specified quadratic irrational within 2e-32 of the stored reference delta. At c = 2N + sqrt(5), with integer N >= 16, unit sin(theta) has sufficient exterior R = 128. These are sufficient radii, not sharp boundaries.

At rational delta = 2h/Q, where h and Q are coprime positive integers and Q > 2h, the tuned pure density and stream series converge for a nonzero finite real odd sine polynomial if the first compatibility condition vanishes and the subsequent affine responses have a positive uniform modulus bound. The first tuning index depends on whether h = 1 or h >= 2. First compatibility alone does not suffice. The common potential may require finitely many logarithms even when density and stream are pure powers. See [119](proofs/119_tuned_rational_series/PROOF.md), [131](proofs/131_general_odd_period_majorant/PROOF.md), [132](proofs/132_general_period_potential/PROOF.md) and [145](proofs/145_general_rational_convergence/PROOF.md).

There are **87 individually certified rational profiles**, all with explicit sufficient radii: 76 two-mode profiles, seven modified Candidate-shaped profiles and four single harmonics. [CERTIFIED_RADII.md](CERTIFIED_RADII.md) records the exact parameters, root ordering, amplitude powers and receipts. V606 checks all 87 certificate hashes and all radius and amplitude transformations in the theory part's table. Necessary top-mode radius bounds are separate from these sufficient bounds.

For every fixed finite real odd sine profile with nonzero first harmonic, the pure series has radius zero on a dense G_delta set of irrational parameters. Its growth limsup is finite at every Brjuno point and infinite densely, so it is discontinuous at every Brjuno point on the full irrational parameter set. This is consistent with stability on bounded Brjuno sublevel sets. See [31](proofs/31_prime_denominator_obstruction/PROOF.md), [32](proofs/32_constructive_divergence/PROOF.md), [42](proofs/42_real_profile_prime_obstruction/PROOF.md) and [297](proofs/297_asymptotic_growth_discontinuity/PROOF.md).

Every fixed canonical truncation at every c > 1 is realized to its stated finite order by an exact exterior solution. The solution and radius depend on the truncation, regularity and weight. No single solution realizing a whole divergent series is proved. Prescribed multipoles and the canonical whole-plane velocity require their separate hypotheses. No global stationary extension is claimed.
