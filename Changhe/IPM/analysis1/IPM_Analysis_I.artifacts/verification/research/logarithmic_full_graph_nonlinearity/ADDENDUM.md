More generally no continuous extension at zero agreeing with Q on D_cp intersected with such a ball exists. A larger normed domain is covered only when it contains these compact tests and its norm on them is bounded above by a fixed multiple of their full graph norm. This correction is substantive to the literal wording, though the already proved sequence establishes the corrected statement without changing its argument.

For the lower bound take a smooth cutoff chi supported in the positive-quadrant B1, equal to one on a neighborhood of the support of a in B0. Disjointness of the four reflected B2 patches lets chi vanish on all other reflected supports. The cross term on the positive patch is supported in supp(a), including its derivatives. Consequently chi times the whole cross term is exactly its positive-patch expression. Bounded multiplication in Y gives ||whole||Y >= ||chi whole||Y / C_chi. The logarithmic norm includes only x>0; the negative-x reflected patches enforce parity and do not add a spurious term to that norm. At fractional orders no disjoint-support orthogonality is used.

The exact joined-normalized background is defined with Sigma(rho)=3/k-1. Hence ell=1/k=(1+Sigma(rho))/3. This is inherited from the accepted canonical normalization, not inferred from the trial satisfying the stationary PDE. For our compact potentials Sigma(h)=0, so both the scalar parameter and all center jets remain unchanged exactly. Expansion of the normalized residual difference therefore has precisely the stated Lh+Q(h) form, even when the individual background residual is outside Y.

The modulation argument first gives the lower bound for all sufficiently large integers n. Increasing C to absorb the finitely many smaller n proves cn-C for every integer n>=1.
