# Pre-computation arithmetic plan and proof-review resolutions

The coefficients are real. Read the original exact dyadic endpoints from the accepted Arb and iv files of topic144, compute their nonempty intersection exactly, and use its exact midpoint and radius. Neither decimal displays nor binary floating values enter this computation. The radius E_Ni is that of the intersection. The half-width h and the particular T_ij are read per cell from topic144 evidence/output_audit.json; use the exact tail_upper_exact field, which already uses the minimum of four proved B_j upper bounds. Do not use the three-cell maxima or displayed floating values.

The direct method uses exact rational local affine composition. The subdivision method also needs a global power-to-Bernstein conversion; the shared conversion formula is explicitly disclosed. Its local interval coefficients then come from three midpoint subdivision levels, a different algorithm. Fix Arb precision512 bits, degree64, eight uniform subcells and the two-box intersection before any range output. Do not change these choices or select mode-dependent partitions after looking at values.

To check every Bernstein coefficient without saving several million long rationals, the direct method saves a SHA256 commitment to the canonical exact coefficient stream for each mode and derivative, along with its eight exact maxima. The checker independently computes global exact rational and Arb Bernstein vectors, subdivides both by midpoint averaging, checks each exact coefficient lies in its Arb ball, and checks its full coefficient stream digest matches the direct method. It also rechecks each saved exact maximum. Algebraic identity of direct and subdivision formulas is proved in PROOF.md; hashes bind the finite recorded execution. Product and coefficient counts describe exactly what ran.

For an exact center coefficient x, convert x.numerator/x.denominator to an enclosing Arb ball. For any ball the upper absolute endpoint gives a valid magnitude bound; maximum lower and upper absolute endpoints enclose the maximum of the exact coefficient magnitudes. Record the largest such enclosure width per chunk. Wide balls are retained honestly; no coefficient is clipped to make a comparison succeed.

The coefficient-radius contribution E_Ni and all angularly weighted final sums are reconstructed independently in exact arithmetic. The final bound uses the verified exact rational Bernstein maxima and radius contribution, together with the inherited exact positive tail. Any comparison to or minimum with the previous bound is performed for the same cell and (i,j); retain the new bound, old bound and chosen value. A selected-cell cap validated this way is no statement about other cells, physical maximum locations, or a global norm.

The first proof reviewer accepted the algebra and requested these explicit implementation obligations. Its real-coefficient caveat is satisfied here. In fact the triangle-inequality convex-hull upper bound also holds for complex coefficients using their moduli, but no complex-coefficient variant is needed.
