# Closure clarification and response to the proof review

Date 2026-10-22T05:45:23.498172+00:00. Read this with PROOF.md. Both conclusions retain their stated ranges. The full proof review confirms the arguments; two remarks need precise adjudication.

The closure clause in (A) concerns the closure of an isometrically embedded graph-norm space, not points outside that closure. Let i:D_test -> H be a linear isometric embedding into a normed space H, and let E be the closure of i(D_test) in H. Define on i(D_test) the linear maps P1(i(h))=h and P2(i(h))=Lh, both taking values in the complete Sobolev space Y. They are well-defined because i is injective, and satisfy ||Pj(i(h))-Pj(i(k))||Y <= ||i(h)-i(k)||H. For w inE choose i(h_n)->w. Each Pj(i(h_n)) is Cauchy inY, so completeness gives a limit. The same inequality shows that this limit does not depend on the approximating sequence. Thus both maps extend uniquely as continuous linear maps E->Y, still with norm at most1. The two scalar component norms also extend, by taking norms of these limits. Consequently a linear isometric embedding of the graph norm DOES automatically supply these continuous extensions on its closure. No separate extension hypothesis is needed here. The referee's assertion that isometry alone does not supply them is withdrawn as applied to this closure; it would matter for an unsupported claim on a larger ambient set outside the closure. The graph-limit pairs need not form the graph of an operator over their first component, and we assert no such fact. Since i(D_0) is dense in i(D_test), their closures inH coincide; inequality (1) has the same valid constants on this common closure, interpreted using P1 andP2.
