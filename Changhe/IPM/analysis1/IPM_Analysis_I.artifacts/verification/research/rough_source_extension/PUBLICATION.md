CLOSURE_NOTES.md proves the following for 61/20<=s<=31/10.

- For compact real Hsigma coefficients, sigma>=s and sigma>3, the auxiliary
  operator has the stated maximal-domain inverse bound
  (1+R/d)^s/(kappa-E), provided E<kappa. This is an existence and uniqueness
  result for the auxiliary operator, not merely an energy inequality.

  e_A in K2 by the product-bound expression (4). The explicit Taylor budget

  and gradient at the origin and tau' L u in Hs near supp tau. Both terms
  of N converge. No characterization of the whole completion is asserted.

  parameter-box, constant-divergence and Hessian budgets. The global Hs
  Hessian constant is gamma_s=[2pi(s-1)(s-2)(s-3)]^(-1/2).
- The rough auxiliary dilation preserves E,a_s,d and carries the same
  maximal-domain identity. The N_(1/ell) inverse bound converts to an Hs
  bound at cost ell^(-s).

first derivatives. It does not hold on arbitrary Hs functions. The odd
