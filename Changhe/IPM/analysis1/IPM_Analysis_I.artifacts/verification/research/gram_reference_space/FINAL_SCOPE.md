# Exact scope of the final H1m finding

2026-10-21. This note is mandatory with PROOF.md and ADDENDUM.md.
The notation J_exp(t0) and J_exp(tmin) means J_R with R=exp(t0) or
R=exp(tmin), respectively. It does not name a source-code function.

The completed review accepts the two analytic theorems. Its assertion
that the central-coefficient domain is dense is not used: no density
proof has been given here. The explicit sequence alone proves
unboundedness and failure of a continuous extension to X. It also
exhibits the sequence criterion for non-closability if that terminology
is used. The final verdict needs only unboundedness.

The reference rejection covers J_code, J_inner, their mutual/principal
cosines, the raw inner-power span, the raw outer-power span, and raw
per-mode spans containing those jumping columns. It is not a blanket
verdict on every spline reference, every tail reference, or every other
field labelled a kind. ADDENDUM.md leaves spline endpoint traces and
exact tail conformity separate. The first and second model responses'
broader phrases about all kinds are read with this restriction.

For any nonzero jump trace b(theta), choose a smooth angular function
phi>=0 supported where b is nonzero and a vector test
nu(theta)b(theta)phi(theta)eta((r-R)/d). The singular pairing is the
nonzero circle integral of b^2 phi, whereas its L2 norm is O(sqrt(d)).
This supplies the sign-independent version of the annular test argument
used for the inner and outer angular columns.

The nine archived JSONs are floating records. The finite SVD is not
recomputed or certified here. Its retained combinations are intended to
satisfy continuity constraints; exact conformity and quadrature control
would be needed for a rigorous spectrum statement. The existence of a
