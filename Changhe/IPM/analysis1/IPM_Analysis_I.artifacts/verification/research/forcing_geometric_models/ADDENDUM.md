# Scope of coefficient projection and derivative use

Written2026-10-21before the finite audit and actual model review.

The conclusion is an inclusion of functions represented by coefficient
boxes and a scalar uniform remainder. It does not assert that every exact
Taylor coefficient belongs to the saved coefficient box. In fact the
independent interval evaluations sometimes extend beyond those boxes;
the projection error is explicitly charged to the scalar remainder.
For each fixed p the projected coefficients are fixed numbers throughout
the rectangle. Coefficients may depend on p; this is sufficient for a
uniform-in-p inclusion, and does not require their continuous selection.

A scalar uniform remainder bound does not imply a bound on any derivative
of that remainder. Thus this result cannot justify formal differentiation
of qb or uq followed by omission of derivative remainder terms. Algebraic
addition, multiplication and scalar analytic composition of function
inclusions can use this conclusion if their own truncation and remainder
estimates are proved. Separately supplied derivative models would require
separate checks. Neither full composed cutoff algebra nor physical
residual derivatives are certified here.

Every block and every p, including the43series fallback blocks, is checked.
Unlike a residual spline model, the geometric function Q_p is defined by
an entire exponential-trigonometric expression, so this particular result
holds on the whole stated rectangle, including any parent overhang.
The source bracket is the exact interval whose binary64 endpoints are
saved in the inherited v2.1 metadata; its relation to the source point is
a separate pinned result of topic115. This result concerns the pinned
contemporary geometry-only replay from topic120. It asserts nothing about
newly published v2.2 arrays or historical v2.1 arrays that were not saved.
