# Geometry review resolutions

The first actual Claude Opus5.5 response accepted the analytic geometry conditionally and listed additional explicit checks. The checker now verifies p_min>R and every floating filter threshold>=R exactly, as well as radial subnode containment and angular subnode containment on the common grid with cap equal to the rational lower endpoint of the cap ball. The first angular hull contains zero. Each distance test intersects its angular box with theta>=0, taking alpha=max(0,lower endpoint); this is valid because only the nonnegative-angle half-disc is under consideration.

The independent angular-cap check uses sin(cap_lower)>R/p_min, with 0<cap_lower<1/2. Monotonicity of sine on this interval is equivalent to the required cap_lower>asin(R/p_min). Both Arb and mpmath.iv evaluate the sine directly. An initial checker run stopped before any distance computation because mpmath.iv has no asin method. The failed source and log are retained; no result was inferred from that run.

The review's concern about possible gaps between rounded float boxes does not describe the frozen producer's derivative domain. Its r_lo/r_hi floats and theta_lo/theta_hi floats are used to decide the mask and print locations. The derivative coordinate factors emt come from Arb balls through MR.from_arb. Its angular factors come from Arb sin_cos on the hull of two node balls. Thus the geometric audit uses the relevant Arb coordinate hulls and independently checks every mask exclusion. It does not require the diagnostic float boxes themselves to abut. The still-open issue is the polynomial-range pairing with those coordinate factors, explicitly excluded from this topic's acceptance.

The outer radial box for each distance test uses the minimum lower endpoint and maximum upper endpoint of independent Arb512 and iv180 exponentials of the exact log endpoints. Both engines then evaluate the same rational radial box. Arb uses the completed-square distance; iv uses (r-p0)^2+4*r*p0*sin(alpha/2)^2 at an endpoint, or 4*p0^2*sin(alpha/2)^2*(1-sin(alpha/2)^2) at the interior minimizer. Each omitted cell is checked against its largest omitted rational delta, which proves all smaller omitted delta cases by exact monotonicity.

Canonical parity can also be checked directly from the specified finite sine expansions: density frequencies are odd and stream frequencies are positive even integers. This avoids adding any unproved harmonic component. Geometric conclusions remain conditional on the named source center lying in the stated bracket.
