# Source conventions for the independent diagnostic

The captured source c2_gram2.py constructs KP with len(A0)=260 angular coefficients and TAU with min(len(A0),len(Bm))=260, although the saved Bm array has261 entries. Its extended Fminus vector has zero coordinates after nF+2 in each selected artifact. We therefore include all260 KP/TAU source angular coefficients and all nF function columns, with their stored original-coordinate coefficients. We do not silently discard the small KP/TAU coefficients. The source c2_vec_profile.py constructs only max(maximum column m+3,Mh+2) angular slots and truncates the KP/TAU vectors to that number. The independent diagnostic reports both this profile convention and the complete Gram-source convention. Their difference, if small, remains floating evidence rather than a certified tail bound.

Polynomial pieces use sigma=t-a for finite left endpoint a, and sigma=t-b for left endpoint negative infinity and finite right endpoint b. Values add when pieces overlap. The derivative is exp(alpha sigma) times (p'(sigma)+alpha p(sigma)). We evaluate monomial sums directly, without importing or executing the producer source or its Horner evaluator. The angular transform uses exact rational coefficients from PROOF.md. Radial rules of orders20 and32 share the finite interval [tmin-25,t0+45] and include all finite source piece boundaries and half-line subdivisions. This is a diagnostic comparison on that finite interval, with no rigorous infinite tail or floating error bound.
