# Response to the first completed review

The review confirms the theorem within its scope. Its "outward-positive"
phrase should mean positive INNER trace. With the usual outside-minus-inside
jump convention, the signed jump is negative. The nonzero measure and X
nonmembership are independent of this orientation. Our statement and bounds
refer to the positive inner trace, not to an oriented jump sign.

The final remedy sentence also needs correction: an arbitrary value-only
repair guarantees continuity, not C4. The exact normalized-coordinate
ideal polynomial is C4. A separately specified higher-order repair would
be needed to claim C4 from general rounded coefficients. Topic132's affine
repair provides C0 only. No transfer of Gram entries or angles is supplied.

The independent endpoint engine uses captured coefficient data, with its
own hexadecimal/IEEE/NPY decoding and direct derivative sums. Its dead
empty-sum initialization is overwritten before any use; it is retained
unchanged because its source and output are already pinned. To strengthen
the source reproduction, a supplemental checker will independently derive
each floating coefficient using exact rational powers and an explicit
binary64 nearest-even rounding algorithm. If it matches, this verifies
that the frozen local outputs equal this separate arithmetic construction;
it still does not authenticate a historical or future job.

The X weight is exactly the topic128 norm, which is used in PROOF.md.
Near0 it is comparable to r^-6; h and r grad h are O(r^3), so the integral
has a bounded integrand before the polar area factor r dr. Both unit-width
references are compactly supported and therefore satisfy the far condition.
The eventual verdict requires every local hash and review gate to pass.
