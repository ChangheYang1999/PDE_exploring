8. The C^2 counterexample

ORIGINAL:
A plain C^2 tail norm is not sufficient: a direction with v''(1 - s) = s^gamma, 0 < gamma <= 1 - beta, makes the integral defining B diverge at first order, with coefficient 1/(2A (gamma + 1) g*^2) - e/(2 g*^3), which vanishes for at most one gamma.

REPLACEMENT:
A plain C^2 tail norm is not sufficient. Choose 0 < gamma <= 1 - beta away from the at most one zero of 1/(2A (gamma + 1) g*^2) - e/(2 g*^3), prescribe v''(1 - s) = s^gamma near the source, integrate from the source and cut off away from it. For sufficiently small nonzero amplitudes, these compatible C^2 perturbations preserve negativity but make the source integral defining B diverge. The displayed coefficient is its first-order coefficient.
