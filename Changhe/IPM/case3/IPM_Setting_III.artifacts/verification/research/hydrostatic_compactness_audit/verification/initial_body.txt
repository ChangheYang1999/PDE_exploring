3.72 Exact anisotropic rescaling by the slope, and the large-slope limit of the canonical inverse ((a) and (b) proposed in the main analysis; (a)-(d) proved by the checker, with numerical checks; (a), (b), the phenomenon of (c) with a second example, and (e) derived independently in an external analysis, which proves (d) under bounds on all derivatives; hand-checked in the main analysis, with a numerical check of the strain formulas of (c); AUDITPLACEHOLDER). Let a0 != 0, s = sgn a0, h = |a0|^{1/2} and eps = 1/|a0|. (a) Put x = h xi, rho(x, y) = a0 g(xi, y), psi(x, y) = h Phi(xi, y) and V = (-Phi_y, Phi_xi) = (U_1/h, U_2). Then rho is a profile of the class with slope a0 and exponent c_l if and only if g is smooth, even in xi and odd in y, satisfies the growth bounds of the class in the variables (xi, y), and
@@    (c_l xi + V_1) g_xi + (c_l y + V_2) g_y = g,   -Phi_yy - eps Phi_xixi = s g_xi,   g_y(0) = 1,   (1)
with Phi odd in both variables and V = o(|(xi, y)|); for each eps > 0 this velocity is unique. The centre strain is unchanged, psi_xy(0) = Phi_xiy(0), so the origin condition reads Phi_xiy(0) = 1 - c_l. If rho = a0 y + b x^2 y + e y^3 + O(r^5), then g = y + B xi^2 y + E y^3 + O(|(xi, y)|^5) with B = s b and E = e/a0, and (T1) of 3.3 becomes 6E = (s - 2 A eps) B. On the y-axis g(0, y) ~ (K/a0) y^p with K/a0 > 0 (3.6), and the representative with L_K = 1 (that is K = a0) has g(0, y) ~ y^p. So the slope is an anisotropy parameter: a profile with slope a0 is a solution with unit slope of the problem whose elliptic operator is -d_y^2 - eps d_xi^2, and |a0| -> inf is formally the hydrostatic limit -Phi_yy = s g_xi. For fixed a0 the map is a change of coordinates, not a symmetry of the profile equation. (b) For c_l > 1 and K != 0, rho = K sgn(y) |y|^p with U = 0 solves the profile equation in the sense of distributions on R^2. It is Holder continuous of order p, not C^1 on the x-axis, violates the gradient bound of the class as x -> inf at fixed y != 0, and is outside the class (its trace K sgn(sin th) |sin th|^p is excluded as a far field by 3.7). It is a formal outer limit of large slopes at fixed K; no statement about approximation by profiles follows. (c) The growth bounds of the class do not control the inverse of (1) as eps -> 0. For 0 < gamma < 1, g = exp(-xi^2) y (1 + y^2)^{(gamma - 2)/2} is smooth, has the parities, g_y(0) = 1 and the density and gradient bounds with exponent gamma, but for s = 1 its canonical centre strain satisfies eps^{gamma/2} Phi_xiy(0) -> -2 Gamma(2 - gamma) Gamma(gamma)/Gamma(1 - gamma/2) < 0, and no velocity of sublinear growth with the parities solves -Phi_yy = g_xi. The external analysis finds the same rate, with the limit constant -2 B(gamma, 3 - gamma) (B the Beta function), for y (1 + y^2)^{(gamma - 2)/2}/(1 + xi^2). These functions are not profiles. (d) One more bound gives the limit. Let 0 < gamma < 1 and let g be smooth with the parities, |g| <= C (1 + r)^gamma, |grad g| <= C (1 + r)^{gamma - 1} and |g_xixi| <= C (1 + r)^{gamma - 2}, r = |(xi, y)|, and let V_eps be the canonical velocity of -Phi_yy - eps Phi_xixi = g_xi. Then |V_eps| <= C' (1 + r)^gamma with C' depending only on C and gamma, and as eps -> 0, V_eps converges locally uniformly to the velocity V_0 given for y >= 0 by
@@    V_0,1 = -int_y^inf g_xi(xi, t) dt,   V_0,2 = int_0^y t g_xixi(xi, t) dt + y int_y^inf g_xixi(xi, t) dt,   (2)
which is the unique velocity with the parities and sublinear growth solving -Phi_yy = g_xi; moreover Phi_eps,xiy(0) = int_0^inf (P_{sqrt(eps) t} * g_xixi(., t))(0) dt -> int_0^inf g_xixi(0, t) dt, where P_a(z) = a/(pi (a^2 + z^2)) and * is the convolution in xi. The same holds for inputs g_eps that converge in C^2 on compact sets and obey the three bounds with a common C. (e) Conditional consequence. Let rho_n be exact profiles with a0,n -> +inf and c_l,n -> c_lim, and suppose that representatives g_n (in the sense of (a)) satisfy the three bounds of (d) with a common C and converge in C^2 on compact sets to g_lim. Then g_lim is a C^2 solution, with the three bounds, of the hydrostatic profile problem
@@    (c_lim xi + V_1) g_xi + (c_lim y + V_2) g_y = g,   -Phi_yy = g_xi,   g_y(0) = 1,   (3)
with V given by (2) and |V| <= C' (1 + r)^gamma, and int_0^inf g_xixi(0, t) dt = 1 - c_lim. Hence, if for every c_lim in a compact interval J the problem (3) has no C^2 solution with these bounds, then for every C, gamma and every sequence (C_R) there is M such that no exact profile with c_l in J and a0 >= M has a representative satisfying the three bounds with C and gamma and |D^3 g| <= C_R on each disc of radius R. Neither the nonexistence for (3) nor such uniform bounds is known for any c_lim, and by (c) the bounds of the class alone do not give the limit of the inverse. For a0 -> -inf the same holds with s = -1, that is with -g_xi on the right of the stream equations. Scope: (a) and (b) are exact statements; (c)-(e) concern the inverse operator and conditional limits; nothing here constructs or excludes a profile. The computed states of 4.7(c) have 2 A eps = 10/a0 between 0.45 and 0.51 and are far from the hydrostatic regime.
