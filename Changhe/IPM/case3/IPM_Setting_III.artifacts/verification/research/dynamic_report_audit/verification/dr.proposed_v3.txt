4.9 Dynamic rescaling in pseudo-time (main computation, with the parts attributed to the checker re-evaluated independently; finite pseudo-time windows and discrete states; not evidence about existence). The time-dependent problem rho_tau = c_w rho - (c_x X + U[rho]) . grad rho of Section 7, item 1, was integrated in the smooth basis of 4.7 with the canonical inverse at every stage, the slope normalization c_w = c_x + psi_xy(0), which keeps rho_y(0) fixed, and a prescribed exponent, c_x = C c_w, so that a steady state with c_w > 0 gives a profile rho/c_w with c_l = C exactly. With p = 1/C, for data rho = K r^p F(th) + o(r^p) as r -> inf whose remainder has gradient o(r^{p-1}), and with U = o(r), these asymptotics and differentiated bounds being uniform on compact pseudo-time intervals, the coefficient K F(th) of r^p and hence the ratio K/a0 are conserved along this flow (since c_w - p c_x = 0), so the flow stays on one slice of the dilations of 3.5 (every profile with K/a0 > 0 has a representative on it), and a start whose trace is proportional to sin th cannot converge to a profile with another trace in a topology preserving the trace, although compact convergence alone is not excluded. Tests: for the manufactured pair psi = -x y/(4 (1 + r^2)), rho = y (r^2 + 2)/(2 (1 + r^2)^2) of the checker the discrete right-hand side agrees with the exact rho_tau to 2.6e-15 at 48 points off the grid and its cubic Taylor coefficients to 1e-11 at 64 x 16 (a test of the unprojected generator on a field with a single angular mode), and the strain identity psi_xy(0) = -a0/4 + int_0^inf rho_3 r^{-2} dr holds along the runs to between 1e-8 and 1e-15. Two implementation defects (a leakage of weakly resolved coefficient directions through product-rule differentiation, and a duplicated moment routine) were found during the computation and repaired; the numbers below are from the repaired code. (a) Constant start. From rho = y w(r) with a0 = 1 at C = 3, the scalars c = psi_xy(0) and c_w agree across the sizes 64 x 16, 96 x 24 and 128 x 32, three cutoffs, two bases and three time steps at exact output times to 7e-5 (relative) at tau = 15 and 2e-3 at tau = 20; between the 96 x 24 state at tau = 20.0470 and the 128 x 32 state at tau = 20.0359, the ratio ||f_96 - f_128||_S / ||f_96||_S on the finite sample set S in 0.1 <= r < 1 is about 9e-5 for the density and 5e-4 for the vertical velocity U_2. The checker's evaluation of the saved states at tau = 20, with its own reconstruction of the density derivatives and its own full-plane inverse, gives a0* = 1/c_w between 60.093 and 60.149 at 64 x 16 and between 60.107 and 60.137 at 96 x 24 over the cutoffs and bases. The annulus 1 <= r < 100 is not resolved: for the saved states at tau about 20.04 the ratios of the sampled norms of the projection defect and of the right-hand side there are 32, 11 and 3.5 percent at the nodes at the three sizes (about 37.5, 12.2 and 4.05 percent between the nodes, by the checker), and the checker's values of the x-derivative of rho/c_w at (6, 8) at tau = 20 are +0.223, -1.002 and -0.489 at the three sizes, so not even its sign is determined there. c_w falls from 0.125 to 0.0166 (a0* from 8 to 60) while at tau = 20 the reported regional sampled maxima of |R|/N_f for rho/c_w range from about 0.018 to 0.62 (checker at tau = 20: maximum of |R*| in the core about 12.2, maximum of |R|/N_f about 0.28): no approach to a steady state is observed for tau <= 20, and the later phase is not resolved. The coefficient of the r^{2 gamma - 1} sin 3th term of the far field agrees with an integrated law derived by the checker to about 1.6e-4 relative or better at the reported times tau = 5, 10, 15 and 20, and the density in r > 1 contributes 62 percent of c + a0/4 at tau = 20. For C in {1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6} the tested starts, constructed from the same coefficient prescription with the physical map changing with C, show decreasing c_w (centred logarithmic rates d(log c_w)/d tau between -0.11 and -0.14 at tau = 5 and between -0.07 and -0.095 at tau = 10), with no arrest or reversal at the reported times. If profiles exist only at isolated values of c_l (as they do locally under the kernel hypothesis of 3.27), this flow has no steady state at a generic C, so this observed drift alone does not decide existence. (b) Starts at the stalled states. Started at the fixed-c_l states of 4.7(c) (a0 from 19.7 to 21.9 at C = 3; c_w approximately 1, c_x approximately 3), the flow leaves them: in an early window the core time derivative grows at a rate of 15.2 to 15.8 per unit tau at the sizes 64 x 16 to 160 x 40 (0.69 a0 to 0.78 a0; for the c_l = 2.5 states, 9.8 to 10.8, that is 0.73 a0 to 0.77 a0), converged in the time step at 64 x 16 (15.49, 15.52 and 15.52 for three steps) and dependent on the cutoff at 128 x 32 (15.5 to 18.5 for cutoffs 1e-8 to 1e-13, alike for both bases at matched cutoff); the first nonpositive c_w step was recorded at tau = 1.1679 and 1.3705 in the 64 x 16 and 96 x 24 local-basis runs at cutoff 1e-8, and at 1.4612 in the 128 x 32 Chebyshev run at cutoff 1e-13; the other listed runs remained positive through their last recorded times, tau = 1.35 to 1.5. In modified flows, which also change the slope normalization: with the velocity, the strain and the rates frozen at the start (a linear transport flow) the core derivative grows by 0.7 to 1.0 per unit tau; with the velocity evolving and the rates frozen the departure is comparable to the full one (13.6 to 15.8); with the velocity frozen and the rates evolving it is much weaker (0.5 to 3.8). The Jacobian compressed to the resolved coefficient directions has positive spectral abscissae 0.73 a0 to 0.78 a0 at the stalled states (one stalled background at 64 x 16 transferred to the larger grids, not three separately refined stalled states), increasing with N at three sizes with no limit established (0.19 to 0.30 per unit tau without the velocity of the perturbation), and 0.47 a0 to 0.53 a0 at the constant start; an independent compression of the checker agrees to about 0.3 to 2 percent in the tested cases. The residuals of the lifted eigenpairs are 0.4 to 9 relative to Re lambda; finite amplification is seen in direct linear integration (12.7 to 14.7 per unit tau for lifted and random vectors), and the nonlinear response to prescribed perturbations is within about 0.1 to 1.1 percent of the prediction of the frozen linearization DF(rho_initial) at the initial state over tau about 0.3, with small-perturbation plateaus of about 1.054 and 0.1042 percent over the tested perturbation sizes (a comparison with the frozen linearization over a short window, not with the variational equation along the moving background). No converged eigenmode of the full discrete or of the continuum linearization is demonstrated, and no instability of the continuum problem is certified. (c) Interpretation (not a proof). The ablations are consistent with a contribution to the departure from the velocity feedback -U[h] . grad rho on the core, where rho_y > 0; for a constant gradient a0 > 0 the local symbol of this term in pseudo-time is a0 k_1^2/|k|^2 >= 0. If exact profiles with a0* much larger than 1 had such an instability (a hypothesis; the stalled states are not shown to be near profiles), forward integration would reach them only from their stable set. This concerns the tested forward integrations and starts; it does not show that every use of the flow to find candidates fails.
