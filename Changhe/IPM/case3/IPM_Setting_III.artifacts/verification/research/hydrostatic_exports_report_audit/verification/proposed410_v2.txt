4.10 Hydrostatic least-squares trial states (eps = 0 problem of 3.72; numerics lane, NUM_REPORT 4P; verifier results 187, 190)

Computed. In the frame of 3.72 (x = sqrt(a0) xi, rho = a0 g, psi = sqrt(a0) Phi) the hydrostatic problem (c xi + V_1) g_xi
+ (c y + V_2) g_y = g, with V_1 = -int_y^inf g_xi dt, V_2 = int_0^y t g_xixi dt + y int_y^inf g_xixi dt, g_y(0) = 1 and the
y-axis tail g(0, y) ~ K y^{1/c} (K = 1), was discretised in the smooth radial-angular basis of 4.7 with radial map exponent
P = c/(c - 1), which fixes the axis tail exponent 1 - 1/P = 1/c. The inverse was evaluated by Gauss quadrature with g_xixi by
central differences (validated on the manufactured field g = exp(-xi^2) y exp(-y^2): V_1 to 3e-15, V_2 to 2e-10; a fixed
step of 1e-5 is round-off dominated where g is large, so the c = 1.5 runs are not used; a radius-scaled step was added), and
the collocation rows at the radial nodes times the quadrant angles plus the normalisation rows a0 = 1, K = 1 were solved by
Gauss-Newton least squares (SVD steps keeping singular values above 1e-13 of the largest) at c = 3 (48 x 12 to 128 x 32) and
c = 2 (64 x 16, 96 x 24), from the simple start g = y (1 + r^2)^{(1/c - 1)/2} and from the re-gauged stall states of 4.7,
with c fixed or free (the map stays fixed, so exact axis transport forces c = c_start: a fixed-tail-exponent experiment, not
a test of selection), with collocated or angularly oversampled rows, and with the centre condition c + int_0^inf g_xixi(0, t)
dt = 1 dropped or imposed as a row (finite-difference row, or the exact axis primitive of result 190, which the lane
implemented independently: on the two centre-kept states at c = 3 and on the free-c kept state the lane's values differ

Scope. All saved states are finite smooth trial densities, reconstructible from the exported coefficient
