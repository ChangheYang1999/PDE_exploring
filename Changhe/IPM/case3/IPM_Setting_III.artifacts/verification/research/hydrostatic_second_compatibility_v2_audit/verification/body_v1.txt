3.76 The second compatibility condition of the far field when 1 < c < 2 ((a), (b) and the statements about f_{3p-2} defined by (1) proved; the expansion that leads to (1) formal as in 3.74(d); obtained by the checker; hand-checked in the main analysis; CHECKERPLACEHOLDER; AUDITPLACEHOLDER). Let 1/2 < p < 1, c = 1/p, D = c - 1 and d = d_2 = 2p - 1 (in the notation of 3.74(d)), so 0 < d < 1. For a function u on R^2 minus the origin that is smooth there, even in xi, odd in y and homogeneous of degree q with 0 < q < 1, put F_u(s) = u(1, s), q_u(s) = q (q - 1) F_u(s) - 2 (q - 1) s F_u'(s) + s^2 F_u''(s), let Q_u be defined by d_xi^2 u(0, y) = Q_u y^{q - 2} for y > 0, and let C_q(u) = int_0^inf s (q_u(s) - Q_u s^{q - 2}) ds, which is finite; for q = p and u = f_p this is the number C_p(Theta) of 3.74(d), and we write C_p(u) for it in general. Let f be such a function of degree p with K = f(0, 1) > 0 and with V[f], given by (2) of 3.72 on {y >= 0} minus the origin and extended by parity, smooth on R^2 minus the origin (by 3.74(d) this requires C_p(f) = 0). Then f_d = (V[f] . grad f)/D, the term of degree d in (6) of 3.74, is smooth on R^2 minus the origin, even in xi, odd in y and homogeneous of degree d, its velocity V[f_d] is given by the convergent formulas (2) of 3.72, and the terms of degree 3p - 2 in a termwise expansion g = f + f_d + f_{3p-2} + (terms of lower degree) of (3) of 3.72, under the assumptions of 3.74(d), give
@@    -2 D f_{3p-2} + V[f] . grad f_d + V[f_d] . grad f = 0,   (1)
since c (3p - 2) - 1 = -2D. Define f_{3p-2} by (1). For every y > 0, (f_{3p-2}(xi, y) - f_{3p-2}(0, y))/|xi|^d -> p K C_d(f_d) y^{p - 1}/(2D) as xi -> 0, so f_{3p-2} is C^1 in xi near the vertical axis only if C_d(f_d) = 0: a termwise expansion of this kind with coefficients C^1 near the vertical axis requires C_p(f) = 0 and C_d(f_d) = 0. (a) For the density f of 3.75(e) (K = 1),
@@    C_d(f_d) = -3 p^2 (4p^2 + 5p - 1) J_d/((p + 2)^2 (2p - 1)(2 - p)) < 0,   J_d = int_0^inf (1 + s^2)^{(d - 3)/2} ds,   (2)
so for 1 < c < 2 the termwise expansion that starts from this density has a third term that is not C^1 near the vertical axis. (b) There are densities f as above with f(0, 1) = 1, trace positive on (0, pi) and increasing on (0, pi/2), f_y > 0 on R^2 minus the origin and f_xi < 0 where xi > 0 and y > 0, with C_d(f_d) = 0, and others with all these properties and C_d(f_d) > 0. So, for 1 < c < 2, neither of the first two compatibility conditions C_p(f) = 0, C_d(f_d) = 0 implies a sign obstruction for positive monotone traces, and both hold together for some of them. Scope: these are conditions on a termwise homogeneous expansion with smooth angular coefficients; no hydrostatic solution is constructed, the conditions at higher degrees are not examined, expansions with angular layers or other matching are not excluded, the densities are not C^1 at the origin, and nothing is claimed for c >= 2, where d <= 0 and the velocity of f_d needs matching to a core (3.74(d)).
