4.x Dynamic rescaling in pseudo-time (variant A).  The time-dependent problem d_tau rho = c_w rho - (c_x X + U[rho]).grad rho, whose steady states with
c_w > 0 are the profiles rho/c_w with c_l = c_x/c_w, was integrated in the smooth basis of 4.7 with the canonical inverse at every stage, the slope
normalisation c_w = c_x + psi_xy(0) and a fixed target c_x = C c_w (so a steady state would have c_l = C exactly and the far-field trace Theta is frozen;
K/a0 is a gauge slice, 3.5).  The discretisation passed an exact manufactured test (the checker's field psi = -xy/[4(1+r^2)]: rho_tau to 2.6e-15, centre
coefficients 0, 9/8, 1/8, strain identity c = -a0/4 + int f_3 s^-2 ds to 1e-8 .. 1e-15) and was repaired twice during the shift (differentiation of the mode
functions instead of the product rule; projected derivative and projection defect logged).  Findings (all finite-window, discrete):
(i) From the start rho = y w(r) (a0 = 1) at C = 3 the scalar diagnostics agree across 64x16, 96x24, 128x32, three cutoffs, two bases and three time steps at
exact output times (c, c_w to 2e-3 relative at tau = 20, 7e-5 at tau = 15); c_w falls from 0.125 to 0.0166 (a0* = 1/c_w from 8 to 60) with the sampled
normalised residual of rho/c_w at 0.02 .. 0.6 by region: no approach to a steady state on tau <= 20.  The core r <= 1 agrees to 1e-4 at tau = 20, the
annulus 1 <= r < 100 does not (projection defects 32 / 11 / 3.5 percent of the right-hand side at 64 / 96 / 128, checker 167; pointwise gradients differing
by up to a factor 2 at (6, 8), checker 173).  The tail coefficient b3 follows the checker's law (154) to 4 digits.  The exterior density carries 62 percent
of the strain c + a0/4 at tau = 20 (I_1 = 0.135 of 0.217).  A scan C in {1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6} shows the same decelerating drift of c_w everywhere
(centred rate -0.11 .. -0.14 at tau = 5, -0.07 .. -0.095 at tau = 10) with no arrest or reversal among the sampled values; as noted in 06:56Z, at a generic
fixed C no steady state is expected if profiles are isolated in c_l, so the drift carries no information on existence.
(ii) Started at the stalled fixed-c_l = 3 states of 4.7(c) (a0 19.7 .. 21.9; c_w = 1, c_x = 3), the flow leaves them with an early-window growth rate of the
core time derivative of 15.2 .. 15.9 per unit tau at 64x16 .. 160x40 (0.69 .. 0.79 a0; 9.8 = 0.76 a0 for the c_l = 2.5 chain), time-step converged in the
slope (15.49 / 15.52 / 15.52 at three steps) and robust in slope to basis and cutoff; c_w crosses zero within tau ~ 1.2 .. 1.4.  With the start's velocity,
strain and rates frozen (a linear transport flow) the core derivative stays near its initial magnitude (growth 0.7 .. 1.0); with the velocity live and the
rates frozen the departure has the full rate (13.6 .. 15.8), with the velocity frozen and the rates live it does not (0.5 .. 3.8), at three sizes and two
steps (modified-normalisation experiments).  The Jacobian compressed to the visible coefficient directions has core-localised modes with Re lambda = 0.73 ..
0.78 a0 (independent of the angular resolution and of the dealiasing, converging with N: 14.43, 15.10, 15.41 on one transferred background) and 0.47 .. 0.53
a0 at the constant start, absent without the perturbation's velocity; the checker's independent compression agrees to 0.25 .. 2 percent (176); the lift
residuals are 0.4 .. 9 relative to Re lambda, so the sign is supported only by direct linear integration (lifted eigenvector 14.0 when resolved; random
perturbation 12.7 .. 13.7, step-independent), consistent with the nonlinear departure within 15 percent.
(iii) Interpretation (not proof): the velocity feedback -U[h].grad rho on the unstably stratified core (local symbol a0 k_1^2/|k|^2 in DR time).  If exact
profiles with a0* >> 1 share the instability (hypothesis; the stall states are not proved profiles), forward integration reaches them only from their
stable set, so forward dynamic rescaling is not a practical candidate-finding tool in that regime.  Two external referee rounds (34 items) and checker
results 167-173, 176 were applied; the late phase tau > 22 of the constant-start runs is unresolved and not read.  Verdict unchanged.
