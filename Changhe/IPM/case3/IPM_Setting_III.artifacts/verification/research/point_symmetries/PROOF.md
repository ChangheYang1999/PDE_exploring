# Complete point-symmetry audit of the normalized differential system

Status: CONFIRMED after independent derivation, 15 exact rational substitution tests and completed actual Claude Opus 5.5 review 13. Date: 2026-10-20.

## Statement and scope

Write ell=c_l>1, u=rho and v=psi. Consider the local differential system

    F=(ell*x-v_y)u_x+(ell*y+v_x)u_y-u=0,
    E=v_xx+v_yy+u_x=0.

Point transformations here mean invertible smooth transformations of (x,y,u,v), prolonged on regular graph jets and preserving the differential system. If ell is also transformed, impose ell_x=ell_y=0. This is a classification of differential-system symmetries, not arbitrary maps of its actual global solution set.

For fixed ell the infinitesimal point algebra is four dimensional, generated by

    S=x partial_x+y partial_y+u partial_u+2v partial_v,
    Tx=partial_x+ell*y partial_v,
    Ty=partial_y-ell*x partial_v,
    G=partial_v.

Every finite invertible point equivalence, including disconnected components and possible changes of ell, is

    z1=alpha*x+d1, z2=beta*y+d2, ell'=ell,
    u'(z)=beta*u(x,y),
    v'(z)=alpha*beta*v(x,y)+ell*(d1*z2-d2*z1)+g,
    alpha^2=beta^2!=0.                                    (1)

For the extended constant-parameter system, alpha,beta,d1,d2,g can depend smoothly on ell. Correspondingly the infinitesimal coefficients can be functions of ell, so the extended algebra is a module, not literally four dimensional.

In the primary full-plane parity and canonical-growth class, the effective action of (1) consists precisely of positive dilations. The two reflection components act trivially, and translations and constant-stream changes do not preserve the required parity/canonical representative. No normalization of ell or a0 follows.

Dependencies: prolongation by the chain rule, regular jet splitting and the fact that a finite symmetry conjugates the infinitesimal algebra. The full-plane step uses the growth and parity hypotheses from PROMPT.md. The source audited was ../analysis/referee/sym1/PROOF.md; every elimination below was independently rederived, including the differential consequences and the possible density-dependent spatial coordinate.

## Infinitesimal elimination, including the mixed orders

Start with Z=xi partial_x+tau partial_y+eta partial_u+phi partial_v+chi partial_ell, with coefficients allowed to depend on all five point variables. Set p=u_x,q=u_y,a=v_x,b=v_y, M=ell*x-b,N=ell*y+a. Prolonging ell_x=ell_y=0 and splitting over F=0 gives chi=chi(ell). The first prolonged coefficients are

    eta^i=D_i eta-p D_i xi-q D_i tau,
    phi^i=D_i phi-a D_i xi-b D_i tau.

The two determining equations are

    (chi*x+ell*xi-phi^y)p+(chi*y+ell*tau+phi^x)q
                           +M eta^x+N eta^y-eta=0,          (2)
    Delta_D phi-a Delta_D xi-b Delta_D tau
      -2[v_xx D_x xi+v_xy(D_x tau+D_y xi)+v_yy D_y tau]
                           +eta^x=0.                       (3)

One must impose F=E=D_xF=D_yF=0, not just F=E. If r=u_xx,s=u_xy,t=u_yy and A=v_xx,B=v_xy,C=v_yy, these restrictions are

    C=-p-A,
    M r+N s+(ell-1-B)p+Aq=0,
    M s+N t-Cp+(ell-1+B)q=0.                                (4)

These are genuine solution jets, not merely formal restrictions. On N!=0 introduce k=v_x and w=v_y. The analytic system is solved in the y direction as

    u_y=[u-(ell*x-w)u_x]/(ell*y+k),
    k_y=w_x, w_y=-k_x-u_x, v_y=w.

Analytic Cauchy data u(x,y0), v(x,y0), w(x,y0), with k=v_x initially, give a local analytic solution by Cauchy-Kovalevskaya; k-v_x remains zero. The freely prescribed tangential derivatives of these data realize every second jet obeying (4). Necessity on N=0 follows by smooth continuation from this open dense jet set.

At fixed first jets and A,B the density Hessian has the free variation h(N^2,-MN,M^2). Its coefficient in (3) is

    (M^2+N^2)(phi_u-a xi_u-b tau_u).

Away from M=N=0, arbitrary a,b force xi_u=tau_u=phi_u=0. Every such a,b is allowed with suitable p,q; the exceptional set is reached by smooth continuation. The trace-free v Hessian remains arbitrary because (4) can be solved for the density Hessian. Its two coefficients give

    xi_x-tau_y+a xi_v-b tau_v=0,
    tau_x+xi_y+a tau_v+b xi_v=0.

Thus xi_v=tau_v=0, xi_x=tau_y and xi_y=-tau_x; in particular xi and tau are harmonic in (x,y). Equation (3) reduces to

    (eta_u-phi_v+xi_x)p-tau_x q
      +(eta_v+2phi_xv)a+2phi_yv b+phi_vv(a^2+b^2)
      +eta_x+phi_xx+phi_yy=0.                               (5)

On N!=0 substitute q=(u-Mp)/N. The p coefficient times N is

    (eta_u-phi_v+xi_x)(ell*y+a)+tau_x(ell*x-b).

Splitting in a,b gives tau_x=0 and eta_u=phi_v-xi_x. With the Cauchy-Riemann relations this implies xi=kx+d,tau=ky+e, with constants on an ell leaf. The remaining coefficients in (5) give

    phi_vv=phi_yv=0, eta_v=-2phi_xv, eta_x+Delta_(x,y)phi=0.

Write phi=h(x)v+beta(x,y), eta=(h-k)u-2h_x v+delta(x,y). The last equation is h_x u-h_xx v+Delta beta+delta_x=0. Hence h is constant, and

    phi=hv+beta, eta=(h-k)u+delta, Delta beta+delta_x=0.

Put T=h-2k. Subtracting (2h-3k)F from (2) leaves

    [(chi-ell*T)x+ell*d-beta_y]p
      +[(chi-ell*T)y+ell*e+beta_x]q
      -b delta_x+a delta_y+ell*(x delta_x+y delta_y)+T u-delta=0.  (6)

At a fixed point with u!=0 the nondegenerate first-jet relation F=0 contains no affine hyperplane: solve for q and split the free a,b,p, or note its irreducible bilinear part a q-b p. Thus the affine expression (6) has zero coefficients. Smooth continuation covers u=0. It follows that delta_x=delta_y=0,T=delta=0 and beta_y=chi*x+ell*d, beta_x=-chi*y-ell*e. Mixed derivatives force chi=0. The final vector field is

    xi=kx+d, tau=ky+e, eta=ku,
    phi=2kv+ell*d*y-ell*e*x+g, chi=0.

Substitution gives pr Z(F)=kF and pr Z(E)=0. This proves both necessity and sufficiency of the infinitesimal claim.

## Why the finite list is complete

The transformed constant equations first give ell'=C(ell), by splitting D_x ell'=D_y ell'=0 over the free first jets. Thus a finite symmetry acts leafwise and conjugates the algebra just established on each ell leaf. Its characteristic derived algebra is n=span(Tx,Ty,G), and [n,n]=span(G). Consequently its transformed spatial coordinates and density do not depend on v, and v'=kappa*v+H(x,y,u), kappa!=0. The transformed density is annihilated by Tx,Ty,G, so u'=f(u). The action of ad S on n/span(G) is -I, which forces the coefficient of S' in the image of S to be exactly one. Therefore u f'(u)=f(u) and f(u)=m u, m!=0. If the point domain crosses u=0, smoothness makes the two one-sided constants agree; on a domain of one sign the same conclusion holds locally. The images of Tx,Ty and S then yield

    z=B X+w u+d, v'=kappa*v+H(X,u), u'=m u,                (7)

where B is invertible.

The apparent hodograph term w u is removed by the same free density-Hessian variation as before. Its contribution to Delta_z v' is

    [H_u-w dot grad_z v'] trace(J^(-T) delta(D^2u) J^(-1)),
    J=B+w(grad u)^T.

For the nonzero rank-one variation from (4) the trace is strictly positive. Thus H_u=w dot grad_z v'. The chain rule, or the rank-one inverse formula, reduces this to

    H_u=(B^(-1)w) dot (kappa grad v+H_X).

At fixed grad u, changing grad v to grad v+t grad u preserves F. Since kappa!=0, the displayed equation forces (B^(-1)w) dot grad u=0 for all allowed density gradients. These contain an open set, so w=0 and H_u=0.

Now the free trace-free stream Hessian in E' forces B^(-1)B^(-T)=s^(-2)I, s>0. The remaining Poisson relation is

    -kappa*s^(-2)u_x+s^(-2)Delta H
                   +m(B^(-1)e1) dot grad u=0.

It gives Delta H=0 and m B^(-1)e1=kappa*s^(-2)e1. Conformality plus this distinguished first direction makes B diagonal: B=diag(alpha,beta), alpha^2=beta^2=s^2, kappa=m alpha.

Let h(z)=H(B^(-1)(z-d)) and U_h=grad_z^perp h. Then U'=(m/beta)B U+U_h. Dividing the transformed transport equation by m and eliminating the original F leaves

    (m/beta-1)u+
      [(ell'-(m/beta)ell)X+B^(-1)(ell'd+U_h)] dot grad u=0.

Freedom of first jets gives m=beta and U_h=(ell-ell')B X-ell'd. Since U_h is divergence free in z, ell'=ell. Thus U_h=-ell*d and integrating gives H=ell*(d1*z2-d2*z1)+g. This is (1). Direct substitution also verifies it: F'=beta F and E'=(beta/alpha)E. Disconnected sign choices for alpha,beta are therefore all covered.

## Restriction to the full-plane class

Write alpha=lambda*sigma,beta=lambda*tau with lambda>0 and sigma,tau in {+1,-1}. At zero translation the two signs act trivially on the required parities, leaving rho_lambda(X)=lambda rho(X/lambda), psi_lambda(X)=lambda^2 psi(X/lambda). Odd-odd stream parity fixes g=0.

For the general component the transformed velocity is U_tilde(z)=B U(B^(-1)(z-d))-ell*d. Sublinear growth alone does not remove the constant. However parity does: if both U and U_tilde are odd under inversion, set e=B^(-1)d. Their two oddness identities imply

    U(T+2e)-U(T)=-2ell*e.

Iteration contradicts U=o(r) when e!=0, equivalently d!=0. This covers translations combined with either dilation or reflection. Thus even an accidental translated representative cannot remain in the full class. Positive scaling preserves all the hypotheses and leaves ell and a0 invariant, as independently proved in ../scaling/PROOF.md. The classification says nothing about uniqueness of profiles with distinct invariant parameters or about unrestricted nonlocal maps of the solution set.
