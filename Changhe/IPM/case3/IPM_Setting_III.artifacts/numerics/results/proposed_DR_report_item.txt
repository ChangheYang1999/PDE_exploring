4.x Dynamic rescaling in pseudo-time (variant A).  The time-dependent problem d_tau rho = c_w rho - (c_x X + U[rho]).grad rho, whose steady states with
c_w > 0 are the profiles rho/c_w with c_l = c_x/c_w, was integrated in the smooth basis of 4.7 with the canonical inverse at every stage, the slope
normalisation c_w = c_x + psi_xy(0) and a fixed target c_x = C c_w (so a steady state would have c_l = C exactly and the far-field trace Theta is frozen;
K/a0 is a gauge slice, 3.5).  The discrete right-hand side reproduces the checker's manufactured field (psi = -xy/[4(1+r^2)]) to 2.6e-15 in rho_tau and to
1e-11 in its cubic coefficients at 64x16, and the strain identity c = -a0/4 + int f_3 s^-2 ds holds to 1e-8 .. 1e-15 along the runs; two implementation
defects (product-rule leakage in the differentiation, a duplicated moment routine) were found by the team and repaired during the shift.  Findings (finite
windows, discrete states):
(i) From the start rho = y w(r) (a0 = 1) at C = 3 the scalars c, c_w agree across 64x16, 96x24, 128x32, three cutoffs, two bases and three time steps at exact
output times to 7e-5 relative at tau = 15 and 2e-3 at tau = 20; the sampled density agrees to 9e-5 on 0.1 <= r < 1 and the velocity to 5e-4 (96 vs 128, times
20.047 / 20.036); the annulus 1 <= r < 100 is not resolved (projection defects 32 / 11 / 3.5 percent of the right-hand side at 64 / 96 / 128, checker 167;
pointwise gradients differing by up to a factor 2 at (6, 8), checker 173).  c_w falls from 0.125 to 0.0166 (a0* = 1/c_w from 8 to 60) with the sampled
normalised residual of rho/c_w at 0.02 .. 0.6 by region: no approach to a steady state is observed on tau <= 20; the later phase is unresolved and not read.
The tail coefficient b3 follows the checker's law (154) to 4 digits.  The exterior density contributes 62 percent of the strain c + a0/4 at tau = 20
(I_1 = 0.135 of 0.217).  Over the sampled C in {1.2, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6} the drift of c_w is the same decelerating one (centred rate -0.11 .. -0.14 at
tau = 5, -0.07 .. -0.095 at tau = 10) with no arrest or reversal; if profiles exist only at isolated c_l (hypothesis, 3.28), no steady state of variant (A)
exists at a generic C, so the drift carries no information on existence.
(ii) Started at the stalled fixed-c_l states of 4.7(c) (a0 19.7 .. 21.9 at C = 3; c_w = 1, c_x = 3), the flow leaves them with an early-window growth rate
of the core time derivative of 15.2 .. 15.8 per unit tau at 64x16 .. 160x40 (0.69 .. 0.78 a0; 9.8 .. 10.8 = 0.73 .. 0.77 a0 for the c_l = 2.5 chain),
step-converged in that slope at 64x16 (15.49 / 15.52 / 15.52 for three steps) and cutoff-dependent at 128x32 (15.5 .. 18.5 for cutoffs 1e-8 .. 1e-13, both
bases alike at matched cutoff); c_w reached zero in three runs (tau 1.17, 1.37, 1.46) and not by tau 1.35 .. 1.5 in the others.  With the start's velocity,
strain and rates frozen (a linear transport flow) the core derivative grows by 0.7 .. 1.0 per unit tau; with the velocity live and the rates frozen the
departure is comparable to the full one (13.6 .. 15.8), with the velocity frozen and the rates live it is much weaker (0.5 .. 3.8); these ablations alter the
slope normalisation and are modified flows.  The Jacobian compressed to the visible coefficient directions has positive spectral abscissae 0.73 .. 0.78 a0
at the stall states (0.19 .. 0.30 without the perturbation's velocity; printed real parts equal for the tested angular resolutions at fixed N and increasing
over three N) and 0.47 .. 0.53 a0 at the constant start; the checker's independent compression agrees to 0.25 .. 2 percent (176).  The lift residuals of the
compressed eigenpairs are 0.4 .. 9 relative to Re lambda, finite-window linear amplification of lifted and random vectors is 12.7 .. 14.7 per unit tau, and the
nonlinear sensitivity to prescribed perturbations is within 0.1 .. 1 percent of the frozen-tangent prediction over tau = 0.3: finite amplification is seen in
the tested integrations; no converged full or continuum eigenmode is demonstrated.
(iii) Interpretation (not proof): the velocity feedback -U[h].grad rho on the unstably stratified core (local constant-gradient symbol a0 k_1^2/|k|^2 in DR
time).  If exact profiles with a0* >> 1 shared such an instability (hypothesis; the stall states are not proved profiles), forward integration would reach
them only from their stable set; this concerns the tested forward integrations and starts and does not show that every candidate-finding use of the flow
fails.  Three external referee rounds and checker results 167-183 were applied.  Verdict unchanged.
