Scope of "must use" statements (A2742-A2745, recorded 2026-11-04T02:07Z). A model that satisfies a specified list of
premises and violates a conclusion shows only that those premises alone do not imply the conclusion; a proof needs
some further property that the model fails. Where an entry below says that a proof or an exclusion must use a named
equation or property, read it in that sense: the named item is one such further property, not the only possible one.
Likewise a formal Lyapunov exclusion means that no nonconstant function on the admissible jet space qualifies;
constants always do.
