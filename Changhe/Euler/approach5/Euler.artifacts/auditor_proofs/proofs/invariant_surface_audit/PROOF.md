# No compact embedded invariant surface for the similarity flow

Let U in C^1(R^3) be divergence-free, c>0 and omega real. Set
B=cy-omega Jy+U. There is no nonempty bounded open set D with positive
volume and C^1 boundary to which B is everywhere tangent. Indeed, the
divergence theorem would give

    0=int_(partial D) B.n = int_D div B = 3c |D| > 0.

Every compact connected embedded C^1 surface without boundary in R^3
bounds a bounded nonempty region, by the separation theorem for such
surfaces. Invariance under the local B flow implies tangency at every
point by differentiation, so such an invariant surface cannot exist.
For a disconnected compact embedded surface, choose one component; its
bounded interior suffices. The statement includes embedded tori. Neither
Euler momentum, decay, flow completeness nor pressure is used.

This is LIT14. It does not address immersed surfaces, surfaces with
boundary, fractal sets, or individual periodic curves. It is independent
of the recurrence theorem for the different vector field Omega.
