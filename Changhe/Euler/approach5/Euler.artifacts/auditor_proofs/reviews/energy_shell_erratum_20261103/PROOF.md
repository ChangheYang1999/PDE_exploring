# Correction to the velocity-shell scope sentence in A1435

## 1. Exact claim being corrected

A1435 correctly proves a conditional small-vorticity theorem for bounded C2 similarity profiles, bounded vorticity, 0<c<2/3 and every finite rotation rate. Its Result sentence says that neither the critical velocity-shell bound nor global vorticity smallness follows from bare decay. The clause concerning the velocity-shell bound is false in the profile class. This correction leaves the theorem and its algebraic constant unchanged.

## 2. The energy implication

Assume U in C2(R3;R3), P in C1(R3) solve the full rotating similarity Euler equation with 0<c<=2/3, div U=0, and Hb: U is bounded and U(R.) tends to zero in distributions. The proved energy law gives U=0 for c<2/5, finite total kinetic energy for c=2/5, and

    E(R)=integral_B_R |U|²<=C R^(5-2/c), R>=2,

for c>2/5. In every case it follows that

    sup_(R>=1) R^(-2) integral_(R<=|y|<2R)|U|²<infinity.

Indeed the shell mass is at most E(2R), and 5-2/c<=2. The finitely sized inner range is bounded by continuity. At c<2/3 the power in R after division by R² is strictly negative whenever c>2/5. The proof uses no derivative bound and no helicity premise. Uniform velocity decay implies Hb by dominated convergence against compact test functions, so it also supplies this shell bound for actual profiles.

## 3. What remains conditional

For 0<c<2/3 the small-vorticity threshold is sqrt(3)(1-3c/2). The energy law does not provide that global smallness premise. A1530-A1536 already give the corrected composition and its strain refinements; their full proof is pinned here. The old A1435 Result sentence must be read with this explicit erratum. Its proof Section9 correctly says that bare decay does not supply global smallness, so that separate sentence is unaffected. No unconditional theorem or new example is claimed.
