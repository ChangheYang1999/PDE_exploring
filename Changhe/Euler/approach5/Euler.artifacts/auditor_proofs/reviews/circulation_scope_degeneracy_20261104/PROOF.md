# The definite-direction premise and an unclassified boundary case

Status: the claimed exhaustive description of the remaining cases is not established by LIT309 or LIT310.

## 1. Exact scope question

Coordinator Note 159 proposes saying that the remaining normalized forms change their angular sign or fail the stated rate along a sequence. The confirmed theorems instead assume a uniform bound zeta.e_theta>=d>0 on a smaller punctured section. They do not prove that failure of this bound entails a sign change. No result in the audited application supplies that implication for every carrier.

## 2. Logical check and correct wording

A nonnegative angular component can vanish or approach zero without changing sign. For example, on a punctured planar disc the smooth form represented by zeta=e_r+sin(theta)^2 e_theta has 1<=|zeta|<=sqrt(2), derivative bounded by C/r and angular component sin(theta)^2. The component never becomes negative but has no positive lower bound. This example is only a check of the logical distinction between the two direction conditions. It is not asserted to satisfy the carrier equations or to construct a completion.

Thus the confirmed theorem leaves open a failure of uniform definite circulation, including angular degeneracy without sign change, as well as failure of the rate when a valid definite-direction reduction is available. The proposed exhaustive classification needs another argument to eliminate the degenerate direction case. Replace the proposed wording by: remaining cases fail at least one stated definite-circulation or accumulated-rate hypothesis; failure of uniform definiteness includes degeneration without sign change.

The source of the actual theorem remains A2607-A2614. The revised wording states only its logical boundary and does not assert that a degenerate carrier or a full Euler profile exists.
