## 3. Why these distinctions are material

A local solution supplies no continuation across the axis, no decay at spatial infinity and no canonical pressure normalization merely by being locally exact. A transported field with a bracket law need not equal curl U. A kinematic Poisson pair satisfies an elliptic identity but need not satisfy the momentum equation. A leading tail may obey only the leading or selected higher-order equations; an error of finite transverse order is not a full solution. None of these examples refutes the main conjecture.

Conversely, a conditional exclusion of a class of actual tails proves vanishing only when its tail existence, regularity, rank, comparison or coefficient hypotheses have been established for the profile under discussion. A bound on one selected ratio, a finite-order jet closure or exclusion of one aligned analytic family does not supply every such premise. The reviewed entries preserve these distinctions.
