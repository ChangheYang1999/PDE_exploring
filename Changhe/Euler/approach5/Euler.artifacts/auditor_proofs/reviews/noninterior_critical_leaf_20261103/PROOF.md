# Noninterior vortical nodes lie over a doubly critical scalar leaf

Status: CONFIRMED at the exact global helicity-free and natural-decay scope below. No critical-node exclusion or second-order scalar flatness is proved.

## 1. Statement and notation

Fix 2/5<c<1/2 and omega in R. Put Jx=(-x2,x1,0), A=cI-omega J, a=1-2c, kappa=1+c and p=(1-c)/c. Let U in C2(R3;R3), P in C1(R3) satisfy

    div U=0,
    (1-c)U+DU(Ax+U)+omega JU+grad P=0,
    U.curl U=0,
    |U(x)|<=C(1+|x|)^(-p),
    |DU(x)|<=C(1+|x|)^(-p-1).

Define B=Ax+U, Omega=curl U and their complete flows Phi,Gamma. Completeness follows from linear growth of B and boundedness of Omega. Let K be the compact forward-bounded B core and E=R3 minus K. Let Xi:E to R3 minus {0} be the actual C1 scattering diffeomorphism, with actual velocity tail V and W=curl V. Set k(z)=(Az).V(z). Let L be its transported C1 extension to O, the complement of the joint vortex core. Normalize the actual pressure limit and put

    H=P-Pinf+|U|^2/2+(Ax).U,
    Q=P-Pinf-|U|^2/2, g=H-Q=U.B.

Suppose z!=0 has W(z)!=0 and k(z)=0, and z is not an interior point of the zero set of k. At y=Xi^-1(z), and on its entire leaf generated by finite compositions of Phi and Gamma,

    H=Q=g=0, dH=dQ=0,
    B cross Omega=aU.                              (1)

The differentials are ambient. If V(z)!=0, U is nowhere zero on that leaf, which is an immersed two-dimensional integral manifold of the independent fields B and Omega. No claim of embeddedness or properness is made.

## 2. A preliminary statement at a regular H point

We first prove a statement that does not use the regular-node exclusion A897. Suppose y in E satisfies Omega(y)!=0, L(y)=0, y is not interior to {L=0}, and dH(y)!=0. Then

    H(y)=Q(y)=0, dQ(y)=0, dL(y)=dH(y)!=0.           (2)

The exact identities used are

    BH=-a(H-Q), BL=-aL,
    Lie_B(U.flat)=-aU.flat-dQ,
    Lie_B(dH)=-adH+adQ.                            (3)

The global helicity-free recurrence result A786-A789 gives dependent gradients of any two C1 vortex first integrals at a vortical point. Since H,Q,L are such first integrals, a regular H-coordinate neighborhood gives

    Q=q(H), L=l(H), q in C2, l in C1.              (4)

This is an ordinary submersion chart. It does not assert scalar coordinates at a critical H point. The actual asymptotic input is

    exp(at)(H composed with Phi_t) -> L in C1 locally on E. (5)

It follows from the already checked actual C1 tail and scattering limits. H and Q tend to zero along every escaping carrier.

## 3. Finite-time continuation of the regular germ

Noninteriority of {L=0}, membership in E and nonvanishing of Omega persist under Phi. At any such point with dH!=0, suppose BH!=0. Then the local carrier passes through an interval of H values about its current value, while L stays zero. Equation (4) makes l identically zero on that interval, hence L zero on an open neighborhood. This contradicts noninteriority. Thus BH=0 wherever that carrier remains regular for H.

Let I be the connected maximal time interval of this regularity containing zero. On I both H and Q are constant, equal to H0. The scalar germs q agree on overlapping H-submersion boxes along the constant-H path, so r=q'(H0) is one finite real constant on I. Let rho=a(1-r) and eta_t=Phi_t^*dH at y. Equation (3) gives

    eta_t=exp(-rho t)dH(y), t in I.                (6)

At a finite endpoint this has a nonzero limit. The finite-time derivative D Phi_t is invertible and dH is continuous, so the endpoint is still regular. This contradicts maximality of I. Therefore I is all real time. Escape and the decay of H,Q give H0=0. This establishes continuation before using any asymptotic covector rate.

## 4. The three possible covector rates

Let beta_t=Phi_t^*(U.flat) at y and eta0=dH(y)!=0. From (3) and (6),

    beta_t'=-a beta_t-r exp(-rho t)eta0.

For r!=0, integration using a-rho=ar gives

    beta_t=exp(-at)(beta0+eta0/a)-exp(-rho t)eta0/a. (7)

For r=0, rho=a and beta_t=exp(-at)beta0. At this fixed escaping point, C1 scattering convergence gives |D Phi_t(y)|<=C_y exp(ct) and |Phi_t y| comparable to exp(ct) at large t. Natural velocity decay implies

    |beta_t|<=C_y exp(-at).                        (8)

If rho<a, multiplying (7) by exp(at) leaves an unbounded nonzero term, contradicting (8). Hence rho>=a.

If rho=a, then r=0, so dQ(y)=0. Equations (5)-(6) give dL(y)=dH(y)!=0, as claimed in (2).

If rho>a, use the local coordinates (4) centered at H=0. Put G(h)=-a(h-q(h))=-rho h+O(h^2). The carrier identities give

    G(h)l'(h)=-a l(h), l(0)=0.

On each sufficiently small punctured side, G is nonzero. Either l vanishes identically there, or scalar linear uniqueness and integration give

    l(h)=C_side |h|^(a/rho) exp(O(|h|)), C_side!=0.

Indeed -a/G(h)=a/(rho h)+O(1). Since a/rho<1, the nonzero alternative contradicts l in C1 with l(0)=0, which requires l(h)=O(|h|). Thus l vanishes on both sides and L is identically zero on a neighborhood, again contradicting noninteriority. The case rho>a is excluded. This proves (2). No finite order for the zero of L was assumed.

## 5. Passage to the critical joint leaf

The diffeomorphism Xi transfers the hypotheses on z to a noninterior vortical L-zero at y in E. They persist on its entire carrier. If dH were nonzero anywhere on this carrier, (2) would imply a regular vortical L-zero there. This contradicts A897, transferred by Xi. Thus dH is zero along the carrier.

Consequently H is constant there; escape gives H=0, and BH=-a(H-Q) gives Q=0. Since Phi_t^*dH is identically zero, its differential equation (3) gives Phi_t^*dQ=0, hence dQ=0. The Lamb identity grad H=-aU+B cross Omega gives (1).

Both H and Q are global Gamma first integrals. Their values and vanishing ambient differentials therefore propagate through every finite Gamma segment, including those meeting the carrier core. The exact affine commutation law

    Phi_t Gamma_s=Gamma_(exp(kappa t)s) Phi_t

reduces any finite composition to a Gamma translate of a carrier point. Hence (1) holds on the entire joint leaf. No continuity of L through a point outside its domain is used.

If V(z)!=0, U(y) cannot vanish. Otherwise (3), with dQ=0 along the carrier, gives beta_t=exp(-at)beta0=0. Invertibility of D Phi_t makes U zero along the carrier, so its actual tail value V(Xi y) is zero, a contradiction. Equation (1) now makes B and Omega independent at y. Their independence persists under both flows. For Gamma this follows from

    D Gamma_s B=B(Gamma_s y)-kappa s Omega(Gamma_s y),
    D Gamma_s Omega=Omega(Gamma_s y).

For Phi it follows from its Cauchy transport and D Phi_t B=B(Phi_t y). Thus U stays nonzero on the leaf. The bracket [B,Omega]=-kappa Omega makes this C1 rank-two distribution involutive, giving the immersed integral leaf.
