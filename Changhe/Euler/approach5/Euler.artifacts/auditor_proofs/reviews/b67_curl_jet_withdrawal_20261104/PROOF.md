## 2. What differentiation proves

Differentiate aV=X cross W at w in an arbitrary direction h. Since W(w)=0 and DV(w)=0, the result is X(w) cross DW(w)h=0. Because X(w) is nonzero, the range of DW(w) is contained in its span. Therefore a covector ell exists with

    DW(w)=X(w) tensor ell.

The identity div(curl V)=0 holds for C2 V. Taking traces gives ell(X(w))=0. Thus ell lies in the annihilator of X(w), identified by the Euclidean inner product with its perpendicular plane. This condition alone does not set ell to zero: the annihilator of a nonzero vector in R3 has dimension two.

This linear-algebra observation is not a construction of a full tail with nonzero ell. It identifies the exact content of the differentiated identity. A separate reduced-equation construction is available in the adjacent pending review, but no result from that pending construction is needed to verify the source repair.
