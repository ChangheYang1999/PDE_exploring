## 1. Exact logical question

If a displayed example satisfies hypotheses H and violates conclusion C, then H alone does not imply C. An argument proving C must use a further property that excludes this example. This does not identify a unique further property, and it does not prove that an untried consequence of a stronger set of hypotheses cannot establish C. If no example satisfies all of H, an unsuccessful calculation under H does not prove that H cannot imply C.

The full before/after hashes and changed passages are recorded in verification. The CLAIMS comparison is restricted to the C65 row. Its other status rows were checked separately in A2680 and are not recertified here.

## 4. A remaining overstatement in ALPHA_STAR

The replacement after Remark 5.6 still says that the restrictions and averages alone do not give an exclusion of alpha-star or branch V, followed by a claim that a stronger argument needs additional input. Read as a mathematical impossibility claim, this is not established by the cited results. A240 already keeps completeness assertions open: affine zero-entropy examples do not test a positive-entropy measure. A271 proves the exact cohomological reduction, not realizability or the insufficiency of D1 on a vortical regular attractor.

The same source's Proposition 5.9 correction explicitly says that the remaining D1 period could itself obstruct such an attractor and that this is unresolved. F17 records that no volume-coordinate solution enforcing all the required periods was obtained. Thus no counterexample with all the relevant restrictions has been supplied. The new unconditional sentence does not follow, although it is weaker than its predecessor.

A precise replacement is: These restrictions and averages have not yielded an exclusion of alpha-star or branch V here. Whether the D1 condition itself obstructs a vortical regular attractor remains unresolved; further analysis of these identities or additional input may be needed. This preserves the proved loop formulation and all open questions. No claim that a D1 obstruction exists is made either.

## 5. A small precision repair in MODE_IDENTITIES

The changed strategy sentence should therefore say no nonconstant monotone quantity of this pointwise kind exists. Its new restriction to this particular class of pointwise functions correctly removes the earlier claim about all possible proofs. The same qualification applies to the references to the first-order result. This is a literal wording repair, not a defect in the classification proof.
