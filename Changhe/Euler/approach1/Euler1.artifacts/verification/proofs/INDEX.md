# Reading order

1. [Profile identities](profile-identities/PROOF.md): equations, residual identities, coordinates, gauges, symmetry, energy scaling and tail balances.
2. [Seed family](seed-family/PROOF.md): exact affine calibration and the localized potential seed.
3. [Local geometry](local-geometry/PROOF.md): conditional axis and peak-vorticity constraints.
4. [Whole-space integrals](whole-space-integrals/PROOF.md): Newton pressure and Biot-Savart reconstruction, signs and quadrature.
5. [Countable stagnation sets](stagnation-obstruction/PROOF.md): independent proof of the conditional exact obstruction below one half.
6. [Radial-feature saturation](radial-feature-tails/PROOF.md): generic restrictions of an unbounded log input in a tanh network.
7. [Irrotational leading tail](irrotational-leading-tail/PROOF.md): a nonzero smooth natural homogeneous tail cannot have zero leading curl.
8. [Bounded-feature expansion](bounded-tail-expansion/PROOF.md): v2 permits angular and fractional corrections but still requires cancellation of the degree d-2 velocity term.

9. [Arbitrary stagnation sets](uncountable-stagnation/PROOF.md). CONFIRMED. Equilibrium-arc capture and a countable cover of local null graphs remove the countability hypothesis; two independent proofs and the actual Opus lead agree.

10. [Ledger bounds](ledger-bounds/PROOF.md): reconstruction with rounded timestamps and checkpoint-crossing step intervals; explicit containment and clock assumptions.

11. [Qualitative residual floor](compact-residual-floor/PROOF.md): a positive but uncomputed floor under fixed derivative and tail bounds and a nontrivial curvature normalization.

Every topic has a standalone informal proof with hypotheses, dependencies and scope. Formal subfolders record that no proof-assistant verification is performed. Numerical checkpoint diagnostics are in ../evidence/ and never prove an exact profile.
