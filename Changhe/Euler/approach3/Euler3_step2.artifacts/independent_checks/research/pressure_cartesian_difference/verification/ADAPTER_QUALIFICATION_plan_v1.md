# Cartesian adapter development qualification

The final adapter plan defines the fresh geometry and remains the scientific protocol. This qualification replays the existing S01 Cartesian check with the legacy geometry, primary artifacts/holdout_S01_v5_cpu.json, seed 2026102929, and pressure orders 16/48/96 and 24/64/128. The seed is a development record; legacy geometry does not use it for points. The 730 completed preflight checks cover fresh seeded geometry, exact stencil algebra, manufactured linear pressure, invalid inputs and final test-cohort refusal.

Before execution, hash this plan, the full qualification script, the primary and old Cartesian records, the preflight record and its bound scripts. Check every preflight binding. Run the generic driver with a 600-second limit, then its independent saved-array auditor with a 30-second limit. The serial controller allows 660 seconds for this task, after the P3 matched, odd and origin tasks. No extra busy CPU worker is permitted.

The full replay must complete and the saved-array auditor must pass. Compare the old and new base points, steps, point counts, radius counts, roundtrip distance, controls, all stencil summary and point rows, all pressure-refinement rows, and all saved numeric arrays. Require finite numeric entries and absolute difference at most 1e-12 times max(abs(old), abs(new), 1e-25); shapes and nonnumeric entries must agree. Source paths, metadata, timestamps, hashes and elapsed times are provenance records, not reproducibility targets. The old full Cartesian computation used about 206 seconds, but that observation is not a runtime promise.

Any failed check remains recorded. A successful replay qualifies the generic I/O and reconstruction path on a previously checked state. It does not supply a final-cohort value, a continuum truncation bound, or a certified pressure enclosure. Fresh geometry has its separate preflight evidence; final results still require the genuinely frozen cohort, exact input/source binding and sampling drawn after freezing.
