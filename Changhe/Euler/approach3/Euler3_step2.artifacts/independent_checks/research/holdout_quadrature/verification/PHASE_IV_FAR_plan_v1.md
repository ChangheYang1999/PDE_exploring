# Bound-state far full-pressure adapter

Registered on 2026-10-29 before any new adapter computation. The mathematics and limitations are those of ../PROOF.md and FAR_EXTENDED_S01_plan.md. This is finite quadrature, not a pressure error enclosure. A small ratio is not an Euler solution.

The adapter reads a complete, bound primary holdout record and its coefficient exchange at the recorded physical parameters. It uses its reference core radius times 2^14, 2^17, 2^20 and 2^23. Each sphere has 24 Gauss-Legendre polar nodes and 80 equally spaced azimuths. The full-space pressure rules remain 16/48/96 and 24/64/128. Every target is a radial panel edge; raw moments and the full-source exterior remainder are retained. No harmonic correction or finite outer boundary is added.

Three complete arithmetic paths are compared: binary64 field/source/Green sums, extended field/Green sums using the binary64 source, and fully extended field/source/Green sums. Binary64 and extended nodes differ by rounding, which is recorded. Leading-tail subtraction is algebraic and does not discard its remainder. Source coefficients, quadrature nodes and weights, tail coefficients, field arrays, pressure coefficients, pressure values, gradients and residual arrays are retained in compressed NPZ. The old S01 record occupies about 42 MB in this format; launch requires the side's ordinary disk guard and a fresh storage check.

Legacy geometry reproduces phases .419 and .563. Fresh geometry draws two phases from default_rng(seed).uniform(.01,.99,size=2). The final seed is the first eight bytes, interpreted big-endian, of SHA256 of the ASCII string `far-full-pressure-v1:<vorticity_seed>`. The source binding is frozen before cohort entropy. Legacy geometry is solely for qualification, never final validation.

The saved-array auditor checks input and source binding, geometry against an independently generated binary64 Legendre rule, the complete five-term and split-six-term norms, absolute residuals, finite arrays, all normalized ratios and pressure changes. Undefined zero denominators give null, never zero. It separately recomputes Green coefficients from the saved source nodes and analytic tail in direct sums, and synthesizes pressure using binary64 normalized harmonics. Direct-sum and angular-synthesis comparisons use a global relative norm per radius with tolerance 1e-11 and absolute scale floor 1e-290. This checks arithmetic against the represented quadrature, not continuum accuracy. Scalar metric replay uses the earlier 2e-12 relative rule, floor 1e-290. Direct-minus-stable cancellation diagnostics retain the previously registered 100 eps times individual-linear-norm-sum allowance. Geometry uses absolute direction tolerance 4e-14 and relative weight tolerance 5e-11; area normalization tolerance 2e-14.

Before final use, preflight must pass fresh/legacy geometry controls, zero-denominator controls, corrupt-input refusal and final-test-cohort refusal. A full legacy S01 rerun must pass the new scalar/Green auditor and agree with every unchanged numerical JSON field and array entry within 1e-12 relative, absolute scale floor 1e-25. Record any failure without changing the tolerance after seeing it. The full qualification is bounded by 1600 seconds for the pressure driver and 120 seconds for its audit; the outer serial controller allows 1800 seconds. Successful source hashes bind the final wrapper. A final-state invocation is limited to 1800 seconds and the campaign numerical cutoff.

A binary64-to-extended rho_nl or pi_nl discrepancy above 10 percent selects the extended value for reporting under the existing rule. Undefined comparisons cannot pass. Neither that test nor a small pressure-grid difference certifies omitted quadrature error. If time or storage prevents a final-state check, record it OPEN explicitly.
