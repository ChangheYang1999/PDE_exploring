## 1. green_edge_recurrence/PROOF.md: CORRECT

Checked: with orthonormal Y_lm the Newton kernel 1/(4 pi |x - y|) gives P_lm(r) = (2l+1)^(-1) int f_lm(s)
r_<^l / r_>^(l+1) s^2 ds, so the 4 pi cancels; s^(l+2) / r^(l+1) = r (s/r)^(l+2) and s^2 r^l / s^(l+1) = s (r/s)^l
give the scaled I and O; differentiating, the two boundary terms f(r) r cancel, which gives D_l. Both recurrences
follow by splitting the sums at a or b; the factors (a/b)^(l+1) and (a/b)^l are at most one, so neither recurrence
amplifies. The tail integral: int_T^inf s^(e-1) ds = -T^e / e and, by parts, int_T^inf log(s) s^(e-1) ds =
-T^e log(T) / e + T^e / e^2, which sums to the stated T^e [A + B(log T - 1/e)] / (-e); Re e = -l - 2 alpha < 0, so
e is nonzero and the boundary term at infinity vanishes.
Conditions for use:
(a) targets below T only; any far anchor at or beyond T needs a separate formula, to be stated;
(b) "no s_j equals a target radius" holds for Gauss-Legendre panels; a rule with endpoint nodes would need the edge
    weight split between its two panels for D_l, since the inner and outer weights differ there;
(c) the closed-form tail is exact only for a single power/log term per mode; the represented source has several
    tail exponents (products of the main, B/D and E channel envelopes, with mixed twist frequencies), so each kept
    exponent needs its own term and the remainder must stay in the finite samples or be bounded and recorded.
