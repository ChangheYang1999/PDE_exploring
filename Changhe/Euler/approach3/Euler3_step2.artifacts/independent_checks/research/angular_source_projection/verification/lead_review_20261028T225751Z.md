## Checks made

1. Toroidal term. curl(a(rho) H_l y) = grad(a H_l) x y = (2 a' H_l y + a grad H_l) x y = a grad H_l x y, since
   y x y = 0 and curl y = 0. At fixed r this is homogeneous of degree l in y, so of degree l in n. A Cartesian
   derivative gives 2 a' y_j (grad H_l x y)_i (degree l + 1) plus a d_j (grad H_l x y)_i (degree l - 1). Bound l + 1:
   checked.
2. Poloidal term. Recomputed independently: div(a H_l y) = ((l + 3) a + 2 rho a') H_l; with g = (l + 3) a + 2 rho a',
   grad(g H_l) = 2 ((l + 5) a' + 2 rho a'') y H_l + g grad H_l; Delta(a H_l) = (4 rho a'' + (4 l + 6) a') H_l (using
   Delta a(rho) = 6 a' + 4 rho a'' and 2 grad a . grad H_l = 4 l a' H_l); Delta(a H_l y) = y Delta(a H_l) + 2 grad(a H_l)
   = (4 rho a'' + (4 l + 10) a') y H_l + 2 a grad H_l. The difference gives
   curl curl(a H_l y) = ((l + 1) a + 2 rho a') grad H_l - 2 l a' y H_l, as stated (the a'' terms cancel). Degrees l - 1
   and l + 1; one derivative raises the bound to l + 2. Checked.
3. Products and support: tr(DU^2) and 2 tr(DU DU_p) are sums of products of two components of degree at most d, so of
   degree at most 2 d; a polynomial of degree K restricted to S^2 has harmonic components of degree at most K. Checked.
4. Quadrature: the M-point uniform azimuthal rule averages exp(i k phi) exactly for |k| < M; after the azimuthal
   average only monomials with even powers of n_1 and n_2 survive, giving polynomials in mu of degree at most D = K + L_P,
   which N-point Gauss-Legendre integrates exactly when 2 N - 1 >= D. Checked.

## Remarks

(a) Consequence worth stating in the deliverables: for the represented field, the production pressure has no angular
    truncation and no angular aliasing error; angular refinement can only expose rounding in tables, field evaluation
    and products, and inconsistencies between the DU used by the pressure and the exact DU. Radial quadrature and the
    exterior tail remain the discretization errors of the pressure (relevant to G4 (d) of specification v1.8).
(b) The bound needs the toroidal and poloidal maxima of the actual job configuration. A job whose B or D channel degree
    is raised would need Lf and the grid rechecked; the worker could record d, K and the two quadrature conditions.
(c) The statement concerns the exact source of the represented field; it does not cover the closure patterns or any
    field term outside the channels A to E (none enters U in the production objective as far as I can see), nor the
    float64 source, for which only the tables' accuracy (G5 (c)) and rounding apply.
(d) Parity: for the even-m class, products of even-m fields have even m, so the even-parity pressure mode set loses
    nothing; for the all-mode class the pressure mode set must contain all m (the m_parity setting passed to
    PressureSolver); I did not check the all-mode configuration here.
