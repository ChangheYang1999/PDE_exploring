# First nonlinear coefficient evaluation, version 1

Prepared 2026-10-29 before new computations. Read ../PROOF.md first. This is a finite quadrature check, not a remainder certificate.

Input is an explicit completed primary holdout record tied to exact coefficient exchanges. Recheck all source and input hashes before and after evaluation. Use CPU35, one busy computation, one thread, no GPU, no servers, no JavaScript or TypeScript, no cluster, no writes outside this side. All evaluation runs join the existing serial queue.

Use three sphere grids with (n_mu,n_phi)=(20,40),(28,56),(36,72). Legacy phases are the exact binary64 values of .537+.011*n_mu, matching the frozen reference driver. Fresh phases come from an explicitly recorded unsigned64 seed through NumPy default_rng, uniform(.01,.99). They are not reused for final selection. For a final cohort, derive the seed with the domain label first-nonlinear-v1 from the already frozen vorticity seed, after all sources and the qualification evidence are in the cohort binding. Final use requires the actual frozen primary record and successful qualification.

Compute F and DU0 directly at r=1 with the qualified extended field route. Compute G and DU1 using the auxiliary exterior A,C substitution in the proof. Use lmax=2*leading_degree+6 for the homogeneous scalar source. Refuse nonzero A or C coefficients above the declared leading degree. Reconstruct the pressure from the nonresonant complex harmonic formula. Save directions, weights, F, DU0, G, DU1, source, pressure coefficients and the resulting arrays, using compressed files. Do not save large-radius source tables.

Record F, G, required G, their difference, advection and pressure-gradient surface L2 norms, the defect divided by the required norm and by the sum of the two nonlinear term norms, source reconstruction error, conjugacy/imaginary residue, modal-versus-grid G norm, energy cross coefficient and two independent helicity cross coefficient formulas. A zero denominator is null, never zero or a pass. Report observed changes across all three grids.

Qualification is completed before final use. It compares the auxiliary field with explicit third Cartesian jets at23 seeded points, tests both homogeneous linear identities and twisted scaling at factors1.7 and3.2, tests divergence, and compares the sphere G norm to the coefficient-only formula. Relative tolerance1e-9 for direct jet/auxiliary and spatial identities;1e-10 for modal norm and the helicity surface identity, with denominator max of the involved absolute term magnitudes and1e-25. Scalar replay of saved norms, coefficients and source projection uses relative2e-12 with floor1e-25. Geometry and bindings are checked separately. Manufacture a zero B,D field to test the zero carried norm and a zero F case to test undefined required-normalized diagnostics. Include invalid seed/mode arguments and final test-cohort refusal. The controls do not certify continuum errors or the omitted pressure remainder.

The full replay uses the prior S01 development input and independently recomputes the frozen explicit Cartesian-jet route on the same three grids. It does not execute the old GPU wrapper. Retain all numerical disagreements. If the old route does not agree within1e-9 on the checked normalized scalars, final qualification remains OPEN until resolved. No tolerance is enlarged after seeing a discrepancy.
