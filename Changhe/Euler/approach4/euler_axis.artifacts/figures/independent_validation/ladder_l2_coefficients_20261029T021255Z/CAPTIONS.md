# L2 spectral coefficient decay

The angular envelope is the maximum absolute native coefficient over radial columns at each Legendre degree. Both parities are retained. Element normalizations differ, so these curves are not a physical energy spectrum. Visible tails do not by themselves quantify the differential-equation error.

The radial envelope is the maximum absolute native coefficient over angular degrees. Ball indices obey the total-degree cutoff and therefore contain fewer angular modes at high radial index. The exterior consists of explicit decay powers and is omitted from the polynomial-index figure.

These figures describe the frozen L2 approximation, c_l=0.749, whose independently evaluated Euler residuals fail the acceptance thresholds. L1 and L2 are distinct reoptimized states; comparison is not a fixed-field convergence study.
