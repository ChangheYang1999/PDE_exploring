The abstract describes stochastic three-dimensional stratified Couette stability with viscosity. It is not a statement of the deterministic inviscid two-dimensional B2 profile problem.
2. **2610.28403v1**, Charles Collot, Slim Ibrahim and Lingjun Qian, *Near-blowup asymptotic analysis and analytical extension for the inviscid primitive equations*. Its recorded submission is 20 October 2026, 15:47:57 UTC. The abstract concerns primitive-equation solutions, an on-axis blowup analysis, a logarithmic boundary concentration scale and a radius-of-analyticity bound. The governing system and scaling are distinct from the B2 problem. No transfer theorem has been established here.
3. **2607.19700v3**, Hyungjun Choi, *Self-Similar Solutions of the Two-Dimensional Incompressible Euler Equation from Large Initial Data*. It does not state a smooth backward Boussinesq solution with nonzero temperature jet.
