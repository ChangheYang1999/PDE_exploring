# What three radial map scales can establish

## Statement, hypotheses, and notation

The six literal arrays in this packet are two triples of represented forced fields. Each triple has the same prescribed beta, lambda, target center amplitude a0, angular count K=24, radial interval count Nt=56 and degree five, and the same physical source coefficients and source map. The L=1.5 states are stored original stages, with their own solver histories. The L=1 and L=2.5 states are re-projections followed by re-solves. Their literal center amplitudes differ from the target by about4e-10 to8e-10; target equality is not exact field equality. Histories, tolerances and one trajectory step cap differ, so this is not an equal-budget comparison. The compactifying coordinate is r=L sqrt((1-t)^(-2b)-1),0<=t<1, with b=beta_basis>0. The fields are Theta=x^2F and Omega=xG with the x parity of PROMPT. The B2 transport field uses only the canonical relative inverse of Omega, W=beta X+U[Omega](X)-U[Omega](0). Adding lambda times the stored source velocity is a separately labeled forced diagnostic.

For every L>0 this coordinate covers the entire half-line r>=0. Consequently a change of L changes the positions of the radial collocation points and the approximation space. It is not a finite physical boundary, and three such choices are not three inverse implementations. Finite sampled agreement of scalar mismatches at these fixed parameters does not establish an actual root, exponent, turning point, profile or discretization error bound. Exact nonzero center residuals or exact nonzero coefficients of growing asymptotic residual powers refute the particular represented field as an exact B2 solution. They do not exclude nearby fields or continuum solutions.

## Radial range and the scaling distinction

The function r(t) is continuous, strictly increasing for0<t<1, has r(0)=0 and tends to infinity as t tends to1. Its inverse is t=1-(1+(r/L)^2)^(-1/(2b)). Thus every map scale represents the same whole-plane domain. If its dimensionless coefficients were kept unchanged, changing L would change the physical fields, not merely their coordinate labels. Here even the coefficients are changed. The exact PDE scaling Theta_s(X)=s Theta(X/s),Omega_s(X)=Omega(X/s),U_s(X)=sU(X/s) instead sends a0 to a0/s and the physical source to its scaled source. At the exact normalization level a fixed nonzero a0 would force s=1. Here the fixed source is its full physical vorticity f. Under U_s(X)=s U(X/s), taking curl gives curl U_s(X)=curl U(X/s), so f_s(X)=f(X/s) with no amplitude factor. Fixed lambda and an unchanged physical source velocity therefore require f(X/s)=f(X). For the actual arrays the unchanged nonzero smooth odd source independently rules out a nontrivial PDE scaling: invariance f(X/s)=f(X) with s!=1 and continuity at0 would give f(X)=f(0)=0 by iterating toward0, a contradiction. Thus the tested change of L is not the PDE scaling. Holding dimensionless F/G coefficients fixed under an L change would produce only a dilation of their arguments, without the additional 1/s factors required by the PDE scaling for these divided fields. No gauge adjustment of center velocity or strain is introduced.

## Exact center test and normalized lower bound

Use x=r sin(phi),y=-r cos(phi), and expand G=sum_k D_k(r)cos(k phi). The canonical center strain is c_self=(1/pi) integral xy Omega(X)/|X|^4 dX. Angular integration uses sin(phi)^2 cos(phi)=(cos(phi)-cos(3phi))/4 and gives
c_self=-(1/4) integral_0^infinity (D_1-D_3)dr.
The R2 odd-mode factor and the change of radial variable give D_k dr=b L S_k(t)dt for k=1,3. Each degree-p B-spline integrates to (kappa_(j+p+1)-kappa_j)/(p+1), with inactive coefficients zero. Hence
c_self=-b L/(4(p+1)) sum_j (g_(1,j)-g_(3,j))(kappa_(j+p+1)-kappa_j).
The decay D_k=O(r^(-1-1/b)) and center regularity justify the absolute integrals. The B-spline integral identity follows by telescoping its derivative recursion; its proof and independent controls are in P160. The literal reconstruction is defined by the stored binary numbers and the independently regenerated binary knot sequence explicitly recorded in the certificates. Equality with any unrecorded historical solver knots is not presumed. All these binary archive numbers and regenerated binary knots are rational, so the displayed finite expression is exactly rational. The source strain has the same formula in its unchanged source basis, and c_forced=c_self+lambda c_source by linearity of the canonical relative inverse. No fitted inverse matrix or arbitrary harmonic strain enters this test.

Let a=F(0,0),q=G(0,0),A0=beta-c and m=c-1-beta/2. Smoothness and parity yield the divided thermal and vorticity residuals at the center
RF=-2ma, RG=(beta-c+1)q-2a.
Their corresponding pointwise normalizers are NF=|2(beta-c)a|+|(beta-2)a| and NG=|(beta-c)q|+|q|+|2a|. These follow by substituting W(0)=0 into the exact divided equations, with the horizontal first derivative retained. For the nonzero a and beta>2 used here, NF is strictly positive, and NG>=2|a|>0. The divided residuals are continuous at the center for these smooth center reconstructions; consequently an identically vanishing Cartesian residual off x=0 would force the same divided limit to vanish. A positive exact |RF|/NF is a lower bound for the divided global supremum and refutes the literal field as a solution, regardless of a sampled grid. In the original Cartesian equation the center residual vanishes by the x^2 prefactor, which is not evidence against this divided defect. P163 proves and independently checks the rational calculation and the zero-normalizer conventions.

## Far field and sample scope

The independent inverse evaluation is specified to integrate the full radial source with its analytic tail bounds through a once-subtracted degree-one Green formula. It imposes no finite-box boundary. The independent method is P63, with other physical-space inverse checks already recorded. The represented tail exponents depend on b and the source's own exponent. Exact angular Fourier witnesses, evaluated by the shared JSON certificate interface of P89 and P101, decide whether a nonzero coefficient occurs at a positive residual power. If RTheta(r,phi)=r^p a(phi)+o(r^p),p>0,and a(phi0) is nonzero, then |RTheta(r,phi0)| tends to infinity. The certificate dependencies must justify a finite expansion containing every term at or above the tested positive order and a remainder little-o of that order. Within such an expansion, combine coefficients at exactly equal powers before selecting the largest nonzero one. Lower powers cannot cancel it. Any logarithmic resonance term, if present, must be included and ordered separately; coincidence of two algebraic exponents alone does not automatically generate a logarithm. The cited certificate proofs, not a bare missing-power observation, justify the applicable expansions. Thus a verified nonzero leading witness proves an infinite full-domain absolute residual even when every finite-grid value is small. The certificate is specified to check all imported field and source coefficients rather than assuming missing powers alone determine a coefficient discrepancy. P111 supplies a separate exact finite-part inverse-constant check.

A sampled maximum over the same Cartesian set for two states compares those evaluated states at that set. It is neither a rigorous supremum nor a bound on inverse/differentiation error. Halving an independent finite-difference step and adaptive quadrature error estimates provide numerical sensitivity diagnostics only. The planned new evaluation uses a fixed common physical radial grid, both signs of x, both directions of the invariant axis, and an exact center test. It distinguishes Cartesian residuals, divided residuals, absolute values, pointwise normalized values, forced diagnostics and defining unforced B2 residuals.

## What parameter mismatch spreads cannot prove

A mismatch value m_i at prescribed(beta0,lambda0) has no root-location content without hypotheses on the nonlinear function in a neighborhood. For example, for epsilon_i>0 and s>0, m_i(beta,lambda)=epsilon_i exp(s(lambda-lambda0)/epsilon_i) is positive everywhere, equals epsilon_i at the sampled point and has lambda derivative s there. Thus both the mismatch values and their differences can be arbitrarily small while the shared nonzero sampled slope is fixed and no root exists. An affine or quadratic local example can share the same sampled value and lambda derivative while having arbitrarily different beta slope or curvature, as proved in P197. Dividing a spread by a reported secant slope is a conditional linear-response conversion. A secant is an average slope over its interval, not an error enclosure on the derivative or a root. Root and turning-point stability remain open.

Similarly a lower objective at one L does not prove that this approximation space resolves the far field better uniformly. The unknown coefficients, optimization paths and sampling nodes have all changed. Region-specific common-grid residuals can test the narrower empirical comparison on those states. A finite objective plateau is not a representation minimum, as P215 proves. These distinctions are logical limitations; they do not invalidate the controlled map-scale experiment.
